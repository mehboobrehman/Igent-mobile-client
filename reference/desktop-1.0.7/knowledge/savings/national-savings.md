# National Savings Schemes (CDNS)

The Central Directorate of National Savings (CDNS) sells government-backed savings products through National Savings Centres, some bank branches and its digital app. They are direct obligations of the Government of Pakistan, so there is no bank credit risk. Rates are set by the Finance Division and revised often, sometimes several times a month, broadly following T-bill and PIB yields.

**Always get current rates from query_rates (Igent fetches CDNS rates daily) or savings.gov.pk. Never quote rates from memory.**

## Main schemes (structure)

| Scheme | Who can buy | Tenor | Profit paid | Early exit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Savings Account (SA) | Any Pakistani / overseas Pakistani | Open-ended | Periodically credited | Withdrawals allowed (limits apply) |
| Defence Savings Certificates (DSC) | Anyone | 10 years | Compounded, paid on encashment | Allowed at par; no profit if encashed very early |
| Special Savings Certificates (SSC) | Anyone | 3 years | Every 6 months | Allowed at par; no profit if encashed very early |
| Special Savings Account (SSA) | Anyone | 3 years | Every 6 months | Allowed; no profit on very early withdrawals |
| Regular Income Certificates (RIC) | Anyone | 5 years | Monthly | Service charge on face value, falling each year held |
| Short Term Savings Certificates (STSC) | Anyone | 3, 6 or 12 months | At maturity | Restricted in the first period |
| Behbood Savings Certificates (BSC) | Senior citizens, widows, disabled persons (with investment caps) | 10 years | Monthly | Service charge, falling each year held |
| Pensioners' Benefit Account (PBA) | Government, armed forces and semi-government pensioners (or eligible family) (with caps) | 10 years | Monthly | Same type of schedule as BSC |
| Shuhada Family Welfare Account (SFWA) | Eligible families of martyrs | Not fixed | Monthly | Withdraw any time |
| Sarwa Islamic Savings / Term Accounts | Shariah-compliant window | Open-ended or fixed terms | Varies | Varies |

Minimum and maximum amounts, encashment charges and eligibility rules can change. Check the scheme page on savings.gov.pk before advising.

## Tax
- Profit on National Savings has withholding tax, with a higher rate for non-filers (often about double). For individuals it has generally been the final tax.
- Behbood, Pensioners' Benefit and Shuhada accounts have historically been exempt from withholding tax and Zakat. Confirm on savings.gov.pk.
- The National Savings rate has often differed from the bank-profit rate. Rates change with each Finance Act; check FBR, CDNS notices or recent news (search_news). See basics/tax.

## How to use in analysis
- Compare **after-tax** yields with other safe options (see savings/safe-rates).
- Behbood/PBA are often the best risk-free option for eligible seniors and pensioners because they have been tax-free.
- Certificate rates (DSC, SSC, RIC, BSC) are fixed at purchase. Later cuts do not affect holdings. Lock in long tenors when rates are expected to fall. Prefer STSC or money market funds when rates are expected to rise.
- Real return = after-tax yield minus inflation (CPI from query_rates).

Example (made-up numbers): an RIC at 12% with 15% filer tax nets 12 × 0.85 = 10.2%. At a 30% non-filer rate it nets 8.4%. With inflation at 9%, the filer's real return is about 1.2%.

## Pitfalls
- Encashing DSC, SSC or SSA very early can earn zero profit.
- The DSC "rate" is a compounded yield only if held the full 10 years. Early exit yields less.
- The website's scheme pages may lag rate or tax changes. Check the latest CDNS notices.
