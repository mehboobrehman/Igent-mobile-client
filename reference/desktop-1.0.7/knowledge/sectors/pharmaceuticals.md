# Pharmaceuticals

Pharma is traditionally a defensive sector because medicine demand is steady. For decades, though, profitability was capped by government price controls run through **DRAP** (Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan).

## Example listed companies (tickers)
- e.g. **SEARL** (The Searle Company), **AGP**, **GLAXO** (GSK Pakistan), **HALEON** (consumer health), **ABOT** (Abbott Pakistan), **HINOON** (Highnoon Labs), **FEROZ** (Ferozsons), **CPHL** (Citi Pharma), **IBLHL** (IBL HealthCare).
- Multinationals have historically paid steady dividends. Local players have often grown faster.
- Most pharma companies have low debt and are often Shariah-compliant. Verify each one live, since status changes.

## Pricing regime (the key driver)
- In early 2024 the government **deregulated prices of non-essential medicines**. Essential and life-saving drugs (the National Essential Medicines List) stayed price-controlled, with periodic adjustments linked to CPI and subject to caps.
- Deregulation let companies raise prices on many brands and widened sector margins.
- Risk: political backlash over medicine prices could bring controls back or change the pricing mechanism. Watch DRAP and cabinet decisions with search_news / read_news.

## Other drivers
- **PKR/USD:** most active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) and packaging inputs are imported, largely from China and India. PKR depreciation squeezes margins unless prices can be raised, which is easier for deregulated products. Live dollar rate: query_rates.
- **Regional trade disruptions:** restrictions on trade with India affect some raw-material supply chains.
- **Energy and wage costs.**
- **Interest rates:** relevant for working-capital-heavy local firms.
- **Exports:** some local companies are building export markets and regulatory approvals.

## What to watch
- DRAP notifications on essential-drug price adjustments and any change to deregulation.
- PKR trend and API prices.
- Quarterly gross margins (query_announcements). They show whether price increases are outpacing input costs.
- Corporate actions: several multinationals have sold or restructured their Pakistani units over the years.

## Pitfalls
- Deregulation boosted margins once. Do not assume margins expand forever. Regulatory reversal is a real tail risk.
- Small-cap pharma names are thinly traded, with wide spreads.
- One-off gains from asset sales or exchange gains can distort EPS.
