# Charts and Graphs

Igent draws its charts itself. Move the mouse over any chart to see the exact values. Each chart has a name for screen readers. Charts with several lines have a legend. Past prices do not tell you the next price.

## Price chart (a pop-up)
Click a share's symbol to open it. It works from the **Watchlist**, **My Stocks Today** and **Market Movers** on the Dashboard, the **Stock Screener** results, your **My Money** holdings (stocks and funds), and in **Chat** (the agent writes PSX shares as links).
- **1M, 6M, 1Y, 5Y** buttons set how far back it looks. Igent keeps up to five years of daily prices, but only for shares it has read (watchlist, holdings, daily movers). A share with no saved prices says so.
- **Line** shows the closing price with a filled area and volume bars. **Candles** shows each day as a box: open, close, high and low. Green closed higher than it opened. Red closed lower. The thin line is the high and low. Five years show one box per week.
- If you hold the share, a dashed line marks your average cost.
- Funds only have one price a day (the NAV), so they get the line, not candles.
- **Should I Buy?** and **Fair Value** open those tools for the share. Esc or a click outside closes it.

## My Money Over Time (My Money, Holdings tab)
Cash plus holdings, at your first entry, at each month end and today. Cash is the figure you typed then. Holdings are what you held then, at the price Igent saved for that day (at what you paid where it has no price that old). A second line shows holdings alone. Money you add or take out moves the line, so a rise is not always profit. Update Cash on Hand after each buy or sell. Today's point equals the Total shown above it.

## Where Your Money Sits
A ring of **Safe** (cash, savings, money market and income funds), **Growth funds**, **Shares** and **Gold**. It uses the same split as the Rebalance Helper (app/tools-more).

## Goal graph (My Money, Goals tab)
**Show Graph** on a goal draws your money growing month by month (saved amount plus monthly investing at the goal's return) against what the goal will cost with inflation. Where the lines meet, you can pay for the goal. Both rates are guesses you set.

## Growth graphs (Tools)
- **SIP Calculator**: future value, the same in today's rupees, and what you paid in, by year. The gap between future value and paid in is growth.
- **Pay Debt or Invest?**: the value of paying off the debt against investing, by year.

## Does Your Money Beat Inflation? (Dashboard)
CPI inflation, the 3-month T-bill (the safe rate), the SBP policy rate and the KSE-100's change over 12 months, on one percent scale. The text above says what safe money earns after inflation, before tax. A negative number means plain savings lose buying power. The KSE-100 line appears once Igent holds a year of index levels. **1Y** and **5Y** set the span.
