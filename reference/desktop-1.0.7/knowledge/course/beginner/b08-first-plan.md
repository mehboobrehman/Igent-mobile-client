## The Big Idea

You now know the building blocks: inflation, compounding, risk, safe options, funds, gold, dollars and tax. A plan simply puts them in order. Name your goal, know when you need the money, pick options that match that time, and start small and steady. A simple plan you actually follow beats a perfect plan you never start.

## Explain It Like I'm New

### Step 1: Name your goals

A **goal** is something specific you are saving for, with an amount and a date. "Get rich" is a wish. "Rs 300,000 for a motorbike in 2 years" is a goal.

Common goals: an emergency fund, a motorbike or car, a wedding, Umrah or Hajj, a house deposit, your child's education, retirement.

For each goal, write down:
- **What** it is,
- **How much** it costs today,
- **When** you need the money (your **horizon**).

### Step 2: Adjust for inflation

Prices rise, so a goal that costs Rs 300,000 today will cost more later.

> Future cost = today's cost x (1 + inflation) ^ years

At an example inflation rate of 10%, a Rs 300,000 goal in 2 years becomes about 300,000 x 1.21 = Rs 363,000. Education and medical costs often rise even faster than general prices.

### Step 3: Match each goal to the right options

This is the most important step. **The date decides the product.**

| Horizon | What fits | Example options |
|---|---|---|
| Under 1 year | Safe only | Bank savings, money market fund, short National Savings |
| 1 to 3 years | Mostly safe | Money market or income funds, National Savings certificates, term deposits |
| 3 to 5 years | A blend | Mostly income funds, with a little equity |
| 5+ years | Growth plus safety | Equity or index funds alongside safe options, a small slice of gold |

As a goal gets close, move its money to safer options so a late market fall cannot ruin it.

### Step 4: A simple starter split

Here is **one example** of how a beginner might split long-term money (5+ years) after the emergency fund is done. It is an illustration, not personal advice. Your own split depends on your income, family and comfort with risk.

| Part | Example share | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Money market or income fund | 60% | Stability and steady profit |
| Equity or index fund | 30% | Long-term growth |
| Gold (bar or gold fund) | 10% | Hedge against rupee falls |

A more cautious person might hold less in equity. Someone young with steady income and a long horizon might hold more. Beginners should stick to funds before buying individual shares.

### Step 5: Invest regularly

Set up a **SIP (Systematic Investment Plan)**: a fixed amount invested every month, usually right after payday, by auto-debit. Many AMCs offer this. When prices are low, your fixed amount buys more units; when prices are high, it buys fewer. It removes the stress of guessing the "right time".

### Step 6: Review, but not too often

Check your plan once or twice a year. If one part has grown much bigger than planned, add new money to the smaller parts to bring it back into balance. Checking every hour only tempts you to panic.

## A Pakistani Example

Bilal, 24, works in Lahore. His emergency fund is done and his credit card is paid off. He has two goals:

**Goal 1: a motorbike in 2 years, Rs 300,000** (he will budget a little extra for price rises).
- Horizon: 2 years, so safe options: a money market fund.
- If he saves with zero return: 300,000 / 24 = **Rs 12,500 a month**.
- If the fund earns about 12% a year (an example), he needs only about **Rs 11,100 a month**, because the profit does part of the work.

**Goal 2: retirement, 30+ years away.**
- Horizon: very long, so growth makes sense.
- He sets up a Rs 5,000 monthly SIP split like the example: Rs 3,000 to an income fund, Rs 1,500 to an equity index fund, Rs 500 to a gold fund (where one is available). Later he plans to learn about VPS pension funds.

### Bilal's first month, week by week

| Week | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Track every rupee spent. Write down both goals with amounts and dates. |
| 2 | Check filer status on fbr.gov.pk and set it in Igent's Settings. Check today's safe rates on the Dashboard. |
| 3 | Open an online account with an SECP-licensed AMC. Make the first investment into a money market fund. |
| 4 | Set up auto-debit SIPs for the day after payday. Add holdings to Igent's My Money. Practise the equity part in Igent's Practice tab first. |

Igent can help along the way with education and live data, but the decisions are his.

## Common Mistakes

- Having no specific goal, so saving feels pointless and stops.
- Putting short-term goal money into equity funds.
- Forgetting inflation when setting the target.
- Copying someone else's split without thinking about your own situation.
- Waiting for the perfect moment instead of starting a small SIP now.

## Key Takeaways

- Every goal needs an amount, a date and an inflation adjustment.
- The horizon decides the product: safe for short goals, growth for long ones.
- A simple split across safe funds, equity funds and a little gold is a common starting shape.
- Invest a fixed amount every month and review once or twice a year.
- Start small, start now, and keep learning in the next level.
