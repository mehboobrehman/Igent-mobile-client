# Complaints and safety

Most problems are solved fastest by complaining to the firm first, in writing, then escalating to the regulator. Complaint portals and procedures change. Reach them through the official homepages listed in this note's sources.

## General rule for any complaint
1. Write down what happened: dates, amounts, names, reference numbers.
2. Complain to the firm in writing (email, official app or complaint form). Get a complaint reference number.
3. Give the firm the time its rules allow to respond. Keep all replies.
4. If unresolved or the reply is unsatisfactory, escalate to the regulator or ombudsman below, attaching your evidence.
5. Act quickly for unauthorised trades or missing money. Delay makes recovery harder.

## Where to complain
| Problem with | First step | Escalate to |
|---|---|---|
| Stock broker (unauthorised trades, missing shares or cash, withdrawal delays) | Broker's complaint desk, in writing | PSX investor complaint channel, then SECP's online complaint system |
| Shares missing from CDC account | Broker, and CDC's investor services | PSX / SECP |
| Mutual fund / AMC / VPS | AMC's complaint desk | SECP's online complaint system |
| Insurance, modaraba, leasing and other NBFCs | The company | SECP (insurance also has an ombudsman) |
| Bank (deposits, cards, fraud, transfers) | Bank's complaint unit | SBP's consumer complaint channel and/or the Banking Mohtasib Pakistan |
| National Savings (CDNS) | Your National Savings Centre, then CDNS complaints (savings.gov.pk) | The federal ombudsman for government agencies |
| FBR / tax deduction errors | FBR's complaint channels | The Federal Tax Ombudsman |
| PMEX broker | Broker | PMEX, then SECP |
| Online fraud or hacked accounts | Your bank or broker immediately | The federal cybercrime authority's complaint portal, and the police |

Notes:
- SECP regulates brokers, AMCs, PMEX, insurers and other non-bank financial firms. Find its complaint system from secp.gov.pk.
- PSX handles complaints against its brokers and runs an investor protection arrangement for certain claims if a broker defaults. Check psx.com.pk for current rules.
- SBP regulates banks, digital banks and payment wallets. The Banking Mohtasib handles many individual disputes with banks.

## Common scams and how to spot them
**Guaranteed-return schemes**
- Promises like "fixed monthly profit", "double your money", "no risk".
- Real investments do not guarantee high returns. Only government savings and bank deposits have predictable returns, at market rates.
- Ponzi schemes pay early investors with new investors' money until they collapse.

**Unlicensed "investment" WhatsApp, Telegram and social media groups**
- "Expert" stock calls, pump-and-dump in small stocks, paid "VIP" signal groups.
- Giving investment advice for a fee needs an SECP licence. Ask for it and verify on secp.gov.pk.
- They may later ask you to send money to "manage" your account.

**Fake broker, AMC or trading apps**
- Apps that copy a real firm's name or logo, or "forex/crypto/gold trading" apps not regulated in Pakistan.
- Verify brokers on PSX and SECP lists, AMCs on MUFAP and SECP, PMEX brokers on pmex.com.pk, and banks on sbp.org.pk.
- Download apps only from links on the firm's official website.

**Impersonation**
- Calls or messages from "bank", "SECP", "FBR", "CDC" or "PTA" staff asking for an OTP, PIN, password, CNIC photo or a "verification" payment.
- Regulators never ask you to send money or share passwords.

**Property and plot scams**
- Unapproved housing schemes, files sold for plots that do not exist. Check approvals with the relevant development authority.

## Protect yourself: checklist
- [ ] Use only licensed firms. Verify on the official regulator's site.
- [ ] Pay only into the firm's official client or collection account from your own bank account.
- [ ] Never pay cash to an agent or into a personal account or wallet.
- [ ] Turn on CDC SMS/e-alerts and bank SMS alerts. Register your own mobile and email everywhere.
- [ ] Never share passwords, PINs or OTPs. Use strong, unique passwords and two-step login where offered.
- [ ] Never give anyone discretionary authority to trade for you unless they are licensed for it and you sign a proper agreement.
- [ ] Check broker statements against CDC statements every month.
- [ ] Be suspicious of urgency ("offer closes today") and secrecy ("don't tell anyone").

## Keep records
Keep digital and paper copies of:
- Account opening forms and agreements.
- Transfer receipts for every deposit and withdrawal.
- Contract notes, broker statements, CDC statements.
- AMC statements and confirmations.
- NSS certificates and receipts.
- Gold receipts.
- Tax certificates from NCCPL, AMCs, banks and CDNS.
- Complaint emails, reference numbers and replies.

Store them for several years. They help with complaints, tax filing (guides/become-a-filer) and inheritance. Igent's My Money can hold a summary of what you own and where.

## If you have been scammed
1. Stop sending money. Scammers often ask for "fees" to release your funds.
2. Call your bank to block cards or transfers and try to recall payments.
3. Change passwords and secure your email and phone number.
4. File complaints with the relevant firm, regulator and the cybercrime authority.
5. Keep all evidence: screenshots, numbers, receipts.
6. Beware of "recovery agents" who promise to get your money back for a fee. That is often a second scam.
