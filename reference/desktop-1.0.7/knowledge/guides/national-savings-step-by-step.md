# Investing in National Savings (CDNS)

National Savings products are issued by the Central Directorate of National Savings (CDNS) on behalf of the Government of Pakistan. They carry no bank credit risk. Scheme details, eligibility and tax are in savings/national-savings. This guide covers the process.

**Rates change often. Ask Igent for current rates (query_rates) or check savings.gov.pk. Never rely on an old rate.**

## Step 1: Choose a scheme by need
| Need | Schemes to look at (see savings/national-savings) |
|---|---|
| Money you may need any time | Savings Account |
| Monthly income | Regular Income Certificates; Behbood (eligible seniors, widows, disabled); Pensioners' Benefit Account (eligible pensioners) |
| Profit every six months | Special Savings Certificates / Account |
| Long-term growth, profit at the end | Defence Savings Certificates |
| Short parking, fixed period | Short Term Savings Certificates |
| Shariah-compliant | Sarwa Islamic products (check the current range on savings.gov.pk) |

Ask yourself:
1. Do I need monthly income or growth?
2. Could I need this money before the tenor ends? Early encashment can cut or wipe out profit.
3. Am I eligible for a special scheme (Behbood, Pensioners' Benefit, Shuhada)? These have historically been tax-exempt.
4. Are rates expected to fall (lock in a longer certificate) or rise (stay short)? See macro/policy-rate.

## Step 2: Gather documents
Typical requirements (check the scheme page for the exact list):
- Original CNIC and a copy (NICOP for overseas Pakistanis, where allowed).
- Nominee's name, CNIC number and relationship.
- For Behbood: proof of eligibility (age from CNIC, widow or disability documents).
- For Pensioners' Benefit: pension documents.
- Bank account details if profit will be credited to a bank account.
- Your NTN / filer status, since it affects tax.
- A source of funds declaration for larger amounts.

## Step 3: Choose how to invest
CDNS has offered several channels over time. Check savings.gov.pk for the current options.
- **National Savings Centres:** visit a centre, fill the application form, and pay by cheque, pay order or another method the centre accepts.
- **Digital channels:** CDNS has introduced online and app-based investing for some products. Check savings.gov.pk for the official app and which schemes it supports. Download only from links on savings.gov.pk.
- **Bank branches:** some products have been sold through certain banks. Check current arrangements on savings.gov.pk.

## Step 4: Make the investment
1. Fill the application form or online form for the chosen scheme.
2. Enter an amount within the scheme's current minimum and maximum limits.
3. Add your nominee.
4. Pay through the official channel only. Keep the receipt.
5. Receive the certificate, account book or electronic confirmation.
6. Check your name, CNIC, amount, date, scheme and nominee. Report errors at once.

## Step 5: Receive profit
- Profit is paid on the scheme's schedule: monthly, six-monthly or at maturity.
- It may be credited to your National Savings account or a linked bank account, depending on the scheme and channel.
- Tax (and Zakat, unless you filed an exemption) is deducted before payment.
- Certificate rates are fixed at purchase. Later rate changes do not affect existing certificates. Savings accounts reprice.

Example (made-up numbers): Rs 1,000,000 in a monthly-income certificate at 12% a year gives about Rs 10,000 a month before tax. If tax is 15%, you receive about Rs 8,500.

## Step 6: Encashment (getting your money back)
1. Visit the issuing centre, or use the digital channel if your product supports it.
2. Bring your CNIC and the certificate or account documents.
3. Fill the encashment form.
4. For early encashment, read the scheme's rules first. Many schemes deduct a service charge or pay reduced or no profit if encashed early.
5. At maturity, encash or reinvest at the then-current rate. Compare first with other safe options (savings/safe-rates).

## Step 7: Nomination and succession
- Always name a nominee. It makes claims easier for your family.
- You can usually change the nominee by filling a form at the centre.
- On the holder's death, the nominee or legal heirs claim through CDNS with the death certificate and the documents CDNS asks for.
- Tell your family where your certificates and records are kept.

## Step 8: Tax
- Withholding tax is deducted from profit. Non-filers pay a higher rate.
- Some schemes for seniors, pensioners and families of martyrs have historically been exempt. Confirm on savings.gov.pk.
- Declare your NSS holdings and profit in your return and wealth statement. Ask CDNS for a tax deduction certificate.
- See basics/tax and guides/become-a-filer.

## Step 9: Keep certificates safe
- Keep paper certificates in a safe place, such as a locker. Keep photocopies or scans separately.
- List each certificate's number, date, amount and maturity (Igent's My Money is a good place).
- Report a lost certificate to the issuing centre at once. Duplicate issue has a procedure and can take time.
- Never sign blank encashment forms or hand certificates to an agent.

## Checklist
- [ ] Scheme matches my need and eligibility
- [ ] Current rate checked on savings.gov.pk or with Igent
- [ ] Early encashment rules understood
- [ ] Nominee added
- [ ] Receipt and certificate details checked and stored

## Red flags
- Someone offers to "invest in National Savings for you" for a fee or a higher rate.
- A rate higher than the one published on savings.gov.pk.
- An app or website not linked from savings.gov.pk.
- Requests to pay into a personal bank account or mobile wallet.

See guides/complaints-and-safety if something goes wrong.
