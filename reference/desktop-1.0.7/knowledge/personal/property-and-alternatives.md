# Property, prize bonds, committees, dollars, gold at home and crypto

Many Pakistani families keep savings outside markets. Each option has a hidden return, cost and risk. The test is always the same: does it beat the safe rate after tax and costs? (savings/safe-rates)

This is general education, not personal financial, tax or legal advice. Rules change.

## Property (plots, houses, flats)

**Return sources**
- **Rent yield** = yearly rent ÷ property value. Residential rent yields in big cities are often low compared with safe rates. Subtract vacancy months, repairs, and property tax.
- **Appreciation**: the price rise. Often the main hoped-for return, but it is uncertain and uneven by area.
- **Real return**: compare price growth with inflation (macro/inflation). A plot whose price rose less than CPI lost value in real terms.

**Costs (concepts only; rates change with each budget and province)**
- Provincial **stamp duty** and registration fee on the transfer deed.
- **CVT** (capital value tax) where it applies.
- **Advance income tax** withheld from the buyer and the seller under the Income Tax Ordinance, higher for non-filers.
- Society or authority **transfer fees**, NOCs, and dealer commission (often on both sides).
- **Capital gains tax** on sale, depending on holding period and current law.
- Official valuation tables (FBR, district collector rates) are used for tax and may differ from the market price.

Round-trip costs can take several percent of the value. Short holding periods rarely pay off.

**Liquidity:** low. Selling can take months, and in a slow market you may have to cut the price.

**Legal and fraud risks**
- **Plot files**: a file is a promise of a future plot, often before land is acquired or the scheme is approved. Files can be sold many times over, or never turn into a plot.
- **Unapproved housing schemes**: development authorities regularly publish lists of illegal schemes and warn buyers. CDA, LDA and RDA all urge buyers to check a scheme's status first.
- **How to check**: look up the scheme on the relevant development authority's website (for example CDA for Islamabad, LDA for Lahore, RDA for Rawalpindi), confirm it has layout plan approval and an NOC, and verify the specific plot and the seller's title with the society and land records before paying.
- **Fake or duplicate documents**, disputed inheritance, and encroachment. Use a lawyer for title checks.
- **Benami risk**: buying in someone else's name to hide the real owner is illegal and the property can be confiscated. See legal/kyc-aml-and-market-abuse.

**Listed alternative: REITs**
Real estate investment trusts listed on PSX let you own a share of rent-earning or development property in small amounts, with daily liquidity and SECP regulation under the REIT Regulations, 2022. They still carry property and market price risk. See legal/regulators-and-laws and the PSX REIT company notes.

## Prize bonds

**Ordinary (bearer) prize bonds**
- Sold and encashed by National Savings and banks in small denominations.
- They pay **no profit**. Your "return" is the chance of winning a prize in scheduled draws. Prizes are taxed, more for non-filers.
- The expected return (total prizes ÷ total bonds) is usually well below the safe rate, and inflation eats the face value.

**Withdrawn high-denomination bonds**
- The government withdrew several high-denomination bearer bonds. Holders were given time to encash them, convert them to registered Premium Prize Bonds, or swap for National Savings certificates, with final deadlines.
- Which denominations are still valid changes. **Check savings.gov.pk or SBP before buying or accepting any prize bond.**

**Premium Prize Bonds (registered)**
- Registered in your name against your CNIC and linked to your bank account.
- Unlike ordinary bonds, they pay **profit** twice a year (taxed) plus a chance of prizes in draws.
- Compare the profit rate after tax with other National Savings options (savings/national-savings).

## Committees (ROSCAs)
- A group pays a fixed amount each month into a pot. Each month one member takes the whole pot, by turn or draw.
- **Return is zero.** Early receivers get an interest-free loan; late receivers lend interest-free and lose to inflation.
- **Counterparty risk**: a member may stop paying after taking the pot, or the organiser may disappear. There is no regulator and little legal protection.
- Fine as forced saving among trusted people. A monthly transfer into a money market fund beats it.

## Cash dollars and foreign currency accounts
- **Cash dollars at home** earn nothing and carry theft and fake-note risk. Your PKR return equals the rupee's fall against the dollar.
- **Foreign currency accounts (FCA)** at banks are allowed under SBP rules and may pay a small profit (taxed). See banking/accounts-and-services.
- Compare: PKR safe rates are usually much higher than dollar deposit rates. The dollar wins only if the rupee falls by more than that gap.

Example (made-up numbers): PKR T-bill 12%, dollar deposit 3%. The gap is 9%. If the rupee falls 5% in a year, the PKR option still wins by about 4%. If it falls 15%, the dollar wins.

See macro/dollar for what drives the rupee.

## Physical gold at home
- No income. Return is the gold price in PKR, which combines the global price and the rupee rate.
- Costs: making charges on jewellery, the buy-sell spread at the jeweller, purity risk, and storage or theft risk.
- Bars and coins from reputable dealers lose less on resale than jewellery.
- See guides/gold-step-by-step and macro/gold.

## Crypto
- Igent stays neutral on crypto.
- Pakistan's legal position has been changing. Virtual assets legislation has set up the **Pakistan Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority (PVARA)** to license virtual asset service providers. Check the latest position from PVARA, SBP, SECP and the federal government, and use only licensed providers.
- Risks: **extreme volatility** (falls of more than half are common), **unlicensed exchanges and apps**, fake "trading signal" groups, Ponzi-style "staking" or "mining" schemes, and lost-key or hacked-wallet losses with no recovery.
- See legal/kyc-aml-and-market-abuse and guides/complaints-and-safety.

## Comparison

| Option | Return source | Liquidity | Typical costs | Main risk | Regulated? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plot / house | Rent + appreciation | Low (months) | Stamp duty, CVT, advance tax, fees, commission | Fraud, illiquidity, price falls | Partly (authorities, land records) |
| REIT on PSX | Dividends + price | High (daily) | Brokerage, fees | Market and property risk | Yes (SECP, PSX) |
| Ordinary prize bonds | Prizes only | High | Tax on prizes | Inflation, low expected return | Yes (CDNS) |
| Premium prize bonds | Profit + prizes | Medium | Tax on profit and prizes | Inflation | Yes (CDNS) |
| Committee | None (timing only) | Fixed turn | None | Member default | No |
| Cash dollars | Rupee depreciation | High | Exchange spread | Theft, rupee strength | Exchange companies only |
| FCA | Small profit + rupee move | High | Bank charges, tax | Rupee strength | Yes (SBP) |
| Gold at home | Gold price in PKR | Medium | Making charges, spread | Price swings, purity, theft | Weakly |
| Crypto | Price change | Varies | Spreads, fees | Volatility, scams | Changing; check PVARA |

## How the agent should compare these with the safe rate
1. Pick the safe rate for the same horizon (savings/safe-rates), after the user's tax.
2. Estimate the option's **net** yearly return: income (rent, profit, prizes) + expected price change − all buying, holding and selling costs (spread round-trip costs over the holding period) − tax.
3. Subtract inflation to get the real return for both.
4. Require a **risk premium** for illiquidity, fraud and volatility. Property and crypto need a large one; a committee or bearer bond with zero income almost never passes.
5. Flag non-financial reasons (housing need, family custom) honestly, but keep them separate from the return comparison.
6. Never give current prices, rates or legal status from memory. Use query_rates, search_news and the official sites above.
