**Symbol:** THALL | **Sector:** Auto parts / conglomerate

Thal Limited is in the House of Habib group. It makes auto parts, including air conditioning systems and wire harnesses, as well as jute and laminates. It also has stakes in Thar coal mining and power.

**Earnings drivers**
- Car production volumes.
- PKR/USD.
- Dividends from its Thar coal investments.
- Interest rates.
