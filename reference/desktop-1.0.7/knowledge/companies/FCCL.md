**Symbol:** FCCL | **Sector:** Cement

Fauji Cement is a Fauji Foundation cement maker. It expanded capacity in the North and South, partly by merging with Askari Cement.

**Earnings drivers**
- Cement despatches.
- Coal and power costs.
- Finance costs from expansion debt, which fall when rates fall.
- Retention prices.
- PSDP spending.
