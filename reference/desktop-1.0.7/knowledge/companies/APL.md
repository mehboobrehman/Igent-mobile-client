**Symbol:** APL | **Sector:** Oil marketing

Attock Petroleum is an oil marketing company in the Attock Group, which also includes ATRL and POL. It sells petrol, diesel, furnace oil and lubricants.

**Earnings drivers**
- Fuel sales volumes.
- OMC margins, which the government regulates.
- Inventory gains or losses when oil prices or PKR/USD move.
- Smuggling of Iranian fuel, which takes volumes away.
- Other income from its large cash balances, which depends on interest rates.
