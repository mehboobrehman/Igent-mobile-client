**Symbol:** NATF | **Sector:** Food

National Foods sells spices, recipe mixes, ketchup and pickles in Pakistan. It also owns A1 Cash & Carry in Canada.

**Earnings drivers**
- Consumer volumes and pricing power.
- Commodity input costs, such as chillies, tomato paste, sugar and packaging.
- PKR/USD.
- Performance of its Canadian business.
- Sales tax.
