**Symbol:** FHAM | **Sector:** Modaraba (Islamic financial services)

First Habib Modaraba is an Islamic finance company in the Habib Group. It provides ijarah (leasing), diminishing musharaka and similar financing.

**Earnings drivers**
- Financing spreads, which are linked to KIBOR.
- Growth of its financing book.
- Credit quality.

Modarabas must distribute most of their profits, so this is a dividend-oriented holding. It trades thinly.
