**Symbol:** MEBL | **Sector:** Commercial banks (Islamic)

Meezan Bank is Pakistan's largest Islamic bank. It also owns Al Meezan Investments, a large Shariah-compliant fund manager.

**Earnings drivers**
- Islamic financing and sukuk returns, which follow the policy rate.
- Low-cost deposit growth.
- Its industry-leading return on equity (ROE).
- Bank taxation in the budget.
- Deposit-linked minimum profit rules.

It is often the core bank holding in Shariah-compliant portfolios.
