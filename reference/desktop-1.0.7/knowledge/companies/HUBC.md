**Symbol:** HUBC | **Sector:** Power

Hub Power is Pakistan's largest listed independent power producer (IPP). It owns plants at Hub, Narowal and Laraib (hydro), China Power Hub (coal) and Thar coal assets. It is also expanding into oil and gas exploration and EV and battery businesses.

**Earnings drivers**
- Capacity payments under its power purchase agreements (PPAs), which are dollar-indexed.
- The 2024–25 government renegotiation of IPP contracts: the old Hub plant's PPA was terminated early.
- Circular debt, which affects how quickly receivables are paid.
- Dividends from its associates.
