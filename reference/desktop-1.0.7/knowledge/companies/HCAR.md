**Symbol:** HCAR | **Sector:** Automobile assemblers

Honda Atlas Cars assembles Honda cars (City, Civic, HR-V) in Lahore.

**Earnings drivers**
- Car demand, which is very sensitive to interest rates, auto finance limits and car prices.
- PKR/USD, because CKD kits are imported.
- Auto taxes and duties.
- Import policy for used cars.
- Model launches.
