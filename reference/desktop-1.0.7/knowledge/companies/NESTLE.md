**Symbol:** NESTLE | **Sector:** Food

Nestle Pakistan is a subsidiary of Nestle SA. It sells dairy products (Milkpak, Everyday), water, juices, coffee and cereals.

**Earnings drivers**
- Milk procurement costs.
- GST on packaged milk.
- Consumer volumes.
- PKR/USD, through imported inputs and royalties.
- Pricing power.

The share price is very high and free float is small, so liquidity is low.
