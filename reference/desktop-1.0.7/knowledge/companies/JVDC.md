**Symbol:** JVDC | **Sector:** Property / real estate development

Javedan Corporation develops Naya Nazimabad, a large housing project in Karachi. It is associated with the Arif Habib Group.

**Earnings drivers**
- Plot and house sales and their prices.
- The pace of development.
- Property taxes set in the budget.
- Interest rates, which affect housing demand.

Its revenue recognition can be lumpy from period to period.
