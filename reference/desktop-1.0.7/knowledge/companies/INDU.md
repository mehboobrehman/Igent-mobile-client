**Symbol:** INDU | **Sector:** Automobile assemblers

Indus Motor assembles Toyota vehicles (Corolla, Yaris, Fortuner, Hilux) in Pakistan. It is a joint venture of the House of Habib and Toyota.

**Earnings drivers**
- Car demand, which depends on interest rates and auto financing rules.
- PKR/USD, because kits are imported.
- Auto taxes and duties.
- Used-car import policy.
- Large other income from cash balances, which depends on interest rates.
