**Symbol:** SCBPL | **Sector:** Commercial banks

Standard Chartered Pakistan is the local subsidiary of Standard Chartered plc. It is strong in corporate, transaction and consumer banking.

**Earnings drivers**
- Net interest margin, which moves with the policy rate.
- Low-cost deposits.
- Fee and FX income.
- Bank taxation in the budget.

The parent's stake is very large, so free float is small.
