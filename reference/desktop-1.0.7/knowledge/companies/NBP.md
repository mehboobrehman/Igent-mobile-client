**Symbol:** NBP | **Sector:** Commercial banks

National Bank of Pakistan is the largest state-owned bank. It acts as the government's banking agent, including for treasury and pension disbursements.

**Earnings drivers**
- Net interest margin.
- Its large government securities book.
- Provisions on loans to public sector entities.
- Pension liabilities and related legal cases.
- Bank taxation in the budget.

Its dividends have been irregular.
