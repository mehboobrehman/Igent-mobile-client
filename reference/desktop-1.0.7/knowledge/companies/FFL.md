**Symbol:** FFL | **Sector:** Food (dairy)

Fauji Foods is a Fauji Foundation company. It sells packaged milk and dairy products (Nurpur brand) and has expanded into other food categories.

**Earnings drivers**
- Raw milk costs.
- Sales tax on packaged milk: the budget imposed 18% GST in FY25.
- Volumes and pricing power.
- Finance costs.

It has a history of losses and turned profitable more recently. It is a low-priced share popular with retail traders.
