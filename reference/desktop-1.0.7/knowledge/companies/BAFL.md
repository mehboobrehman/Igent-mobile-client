**Symbol:** BAFL | **Sector:** Commercial banks

Bank Alfalah is a large private bank owned by the Abu Dhabi Group. It runs both conventional and Islamic banking and is known for its digital banking.

**Earnings drivers**
- Net interest margin, which depends on the policy rate.
- Deposit growth and mix.
- Growth of its Islamic banking arm.
- Fee and FX income.
- Provisions.
- Bank taxation set in the budget.

**Shariah:** It is a conventional bank, even though it has an Islamic window. Igent reads current KMI index status live from PSX.
