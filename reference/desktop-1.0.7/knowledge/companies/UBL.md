**Symbol:** UBL | **Sector:** Commercial banks

United Bank is a large private bank controlled by the Bestway Group. It has international operations and absorbed Silk Bank in 2025 (check recent announcements for completion).

**Earnings drivers**
- Net interest margin.
- A large PIB book: when rates fall, fixed-rate bonds gain value, which has boosted its earnings and book value.
- Deposit growth.
- Its international branches.
- Bank taxation in the budget.
