**Symbol:** AHCL | **Sector:** Investment / holding company

Arif Habib Corporation is the listed holding company of the Arif Habib Group. Its stakes span brokerage (Arif Habib Ltd), fertilizer (Fatima Fertilizer), real estate and other businesses.

**Earnings drivers**
- Dividends from its holdings.
- Market-value changes on its listed investments, so it behaves like a leveraged bet on the PSX.
- Property projects.

**Valuation note:** holding companies usually trade at a discount to their net asset value (NAV).
