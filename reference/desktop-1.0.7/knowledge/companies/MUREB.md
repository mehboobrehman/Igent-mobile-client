**Symbol:** MUREB | **Sector:** Beverages

Murree Brewery, based in Rawalpindi, makes alcoholic beverages (beer and liquor) for permitted non-Muslim and licensed sales. It also sells juices, glass bottles and other non-alcoholic products.

**Earnings drivers**
- Excise duties and permit regime.
- Volumes.
- Input costs.
