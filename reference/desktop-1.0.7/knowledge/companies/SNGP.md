**Symbol:** SNGP | **Sector:** Gas transmission and distribution (utility)

SNGP is the state-controlled gas utility serving Punjab and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. It also distributes imported RLNG.

**Earnings drivers**
- OGRA's guaranteed return on its net fixed assets, under a regulated return model.
- Unaccounted-for gas (UFG) losses.
- Circular debt: huge payables and receivables in the gas chain.

Its earnings are formula-based, but its cash flows are weak. Its share price reacts strongly to circular-debt news.
