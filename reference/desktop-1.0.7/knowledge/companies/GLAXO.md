**Symbol:** GLAXO | **Sector:** Pharmaceuticals

GSK Pakistan is the local arm of GSK. It sells prescription medicines and vaccines, including antibiotic brands such as Augmentin.

**Earnings drivers**
- DRAP price approvals and deregulation of non-essential drugs.
- PKR/USD, because inputs are imported.
- Volumes.
- Gross margin recovery.

GSK has been reviewing its presence in Pakistan. Check recent PSX announcements for its current status.
