**Symbol:** PKGS | **Sector:** Paper & packaging

Packages is a packaging and holding company in the Ali Group. It owns packaging operations and holds stakes in Tri-Pack, Bulleh Shah Packaging, Packages Real Estate, IGI insurance and Tetra Pak Pakistan (minority).

**Earnings drivers**
- Packaging demand from food and FMCG companies.
- Pulp, paper and resin prices.
- PKR/USD.
- Dividends from its investments.

It is valued with a sum-of-the-parts approach.
