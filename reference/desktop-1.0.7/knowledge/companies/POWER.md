**Symbol:** POWER | **Sector:** Cement

Power Cement is a southern cement maker based in Nooriabad, Sindh. It is part of the Arif Habib Group.

**Earnings drivers**
- Cement demand in the South.
- Exports.
- Coal costs and PKR/USD.
- Finance costs: it is leveraged after a recent expansion, so it is sensitive to interest rates.

It is a low-priced share traded heavily by retail investors.
