**Symbol:** KTML | **Sector:** Textiles (composite)

Kohinoor Textile Mills is a composite textile company in the Saigol group. It holds a stake in Maple Leaf Cement (MLCF).

**Earnings drivers**
- Textile exports.
- Cotton prices and energy costs.
- PKR/USD.
- The export tax regime in the budget.
- The value of its MLCF holding.
