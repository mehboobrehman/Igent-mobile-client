**Symbol:** ATRL | **Sector:** Refinery

Attock Refinery runs a refinery in Rawalpindi that processes mainly local crude from northern oilfields. It is part of the Attock Group.

**Earnings drivers**
- Gross refining margins, especially the spread between product prices and crude.
- Throughput.
- Inventory gains or losses.
- Government refining policy, including incentives for upgrades.
- Furnace oil demand.
- Treasury income on its cash balances.
