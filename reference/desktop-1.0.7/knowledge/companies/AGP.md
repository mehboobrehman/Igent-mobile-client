**Symbol:** AGP | **Sector:** Pharmaceuticals

AGP is a Pakistani pharmaceutical company. It makes and markets branded generic medicines, some of them under licence from foreign companies.

**Earnings drivers**
- Drug pricing: deregulation of non-essential medicines versus DRAP price controls on essential ones.
- Cost of imported raw materials, which depends on PKR/USD.
- Launches of new products.
- Finance costs, if it has borrowed for acquisitions.
