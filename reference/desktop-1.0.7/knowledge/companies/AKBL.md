**Symbol:** AKBL | **Sector:** Commercial banks

Askari Bank is a mid-sized conventional commercial bank. Its majority owner is the Fauji Foundation group.

**Earnings drivers**
- Net interest margin, which moves with the policy rate and KIBOR.
- Its large investment book in government securities.
- Deposit mix.
- Provisions.
- Budget tax measures on banks.
