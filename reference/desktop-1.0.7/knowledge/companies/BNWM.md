**Symbol:** BNWM | **Sector:** Textiles (woollen)

Bannu Woollen Mills is a small woollen textile maker and part of the Bannu/Lakki group.

**Liquidity warning:** it has a very small free float and a near-zero index weight. It trades thinly and its price can be volatile, so treat it as illiquid.

**Earnings drivers**
- Demand for woollen fabric.
- Raw material costs.
- Energy tariffs.
