**Symbol:** PAEL | **Sector:** Cable & electrical goods

Pak Elektron (PEL) makes home appliances such as refrigerators, air conditioners and LED TVs. It also makes power equipment: transformers, switchgear and meters.

**Earnings drivers**
- Consumer durable demand.
- Interest rates, because it is leveraged and buyers rely on consumer credit.
- PKR/USD, through imported components.
- Orders from power distribution companies.

It is a popular retail stock with high volatility.
