**Symbol:** BML | **Sector:** Commercial banks

Bank Makramah is a small conventional bank, formerly Summit Bank. It has a long history of weak capital and restructuring.

**Earnings drivers and risks**
- Whether it manages to recapitalise and meet SBP capital requirements.
- Clean-up of non-performing loans.
- Whether its turnaround plans succeed.

**Risk note:** this is speculative and higher-risk than the large banks. Its price can move on news rather than on earnings.
