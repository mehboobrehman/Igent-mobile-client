**Symbol:** HALEON | **Sector:** Pharmaceuticals / consumer health

Haleon Pakistan is the former GSK Consumer Healthcare business. It sells over-the-counter brands such as Panadol, Sensodyne and others.

**Earnings drivers**
- Pricing approvals: Panadol was historically price-controlled, and some products were deregulated.
- PKR/USD, because paracetamol API is imported.
- Consumer volumes.
