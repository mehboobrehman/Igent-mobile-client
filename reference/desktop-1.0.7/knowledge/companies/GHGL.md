**Symbol:** GHGL | **Sector:** Glass & ceramics

Ghani Glass makes glass containers (bottles and jars) for beverages, pharmaceuticals and food, as well as float glass.

**Earnings drivers**
- Demand from beverage and pharmaceutical customers.
- Gas and RLNG costs, because glass-making is energy-intensive.
- Soda ash prices and PKR/USD.
- Capacity additions.
