**Symbol:** PSEL | **Sector:** Hotels

Pakistan Services owns the Pearl Continental and Marriott hotels in Pakistan and is part of the Hashoo Group.

**Earnings drivers**
- Hotel occupancy and room rates, which depend on business and foreign travel.
- Security conditions.
- Events.
- PKR/USD.
- Finance costs.

It has very low free float, so its price can move sharply.
