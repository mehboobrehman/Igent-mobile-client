**Symbol:** PSO | **Sector:** Oil marketing

PSO is Pakistan's largest oil marketing company and is state-controlled. It also imports LNG and holds stakes in Pak-Arab Refinery and Pakistan Refinery.

**Earnings drivers**
- Fuel volumes.
- Regulated OMC margins.
- Inventory gains or losses from oil price and PKR moves.
- Circular debt: very large receivables from SNGP for LNG and from power producers, which raise its finance costs.
- Any circular-debt resolution measures, which can trigger big re-ratings.
