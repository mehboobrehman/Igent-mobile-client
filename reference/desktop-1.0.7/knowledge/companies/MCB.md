**Symbol:** MCB | **Sector:** Commercial banks

MCB is a large conventional bank in the Nishat Group, known for its low-cost current account deposit base. It owns MCB Islamic Bank and has an asset management affiliate.

**Earnings drivers**
- Net interest margin, which moves with the policy rate.
- Its current account (CASA) mix.
- Fee income.
- Asset quality.
- Bank taxation in the budget.

Investors hold it mainly for its dividend payouts.
