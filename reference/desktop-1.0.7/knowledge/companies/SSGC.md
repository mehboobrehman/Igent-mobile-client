**Symbol:** SSGC | **Sector:** Gas transmission and distribution (utility)

SSGC is the state-controlled gas utility serving Sindh and Balochistan.

**Earnings drivers**
- OGRA's regulated return on its assets.
- Unaccounted-for gas (UFG) losses, which are high and partly disallowed by the regulator.
- Circular debt.
- Its LPG and other subsidiaries.

It has a history of losses and weak cash flows. It is a speculative stock driven by circular-debt and privatisation news.
