**Symbol:** PPL | **Sector:** Oil & gas exploration and production

Pakistan Petroleum is a state-controlled, gas-heavy exploration and production company. Sui is its legacy field.

**Earnings drivers**
- Gas production and wellhead pricing.
- Oil prices and PKR/USD.
- Circular debt: large receivables from the gas utilities hold back its dividends.
- Reko Diq mining stake.
- Exploration success.
