# Who regulates what, and the main laws

**General information only, not legal advice.** Laws and rules change. For your own case, check the official source or ask a licensed lawyer or adviser.

How to complain to these bodies is covered in guides/complaints-and-safety. This note explains who does what.

## The bodies and their roles
| Body | What it is | What it does for you |
|---|---|---|
| **SECP** (Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan) | Main regulator for capital markets and non-bank finance | Licenses and supervises PSX, CDC, NCCPL, PMEX, brokers, AMCs, pension fund managers, insurers, modarabas, leasing and other NBFCs, credit rating companies and investment advisers. Also registers companies. |
| **SBP** (State Bank of Pakistan) | Central bank | Regulates banks, microfinance banks, digital banks, payment wallets and exchange companies. Runs monetary policy and foreign exchange rules. |
| **PSX** (Pakistan Stock Exchange) | The only stock exchange, regulated by SECP | Runs trading, lists companies, sets rules for its brokers (TREC holders), handles investor complaints against brokers. See basics/psx. |
| **CDC** (Central Depository Company) | Central depository | Holds shares and other securities electronically, in your CDC sub-account or Investor Account. |
| **NCCPL** (National Clearing Company of Pakistan) | Clearing house | Clears and settles trades, issues your UIN, computes capital gains tax on listed shares. |
| **PMEX** (Pakistan Mercantile Exchange) | Futures and commodity exchange, regulated by SECP | Trading in gold, oil, currency and other futures through PMEX brokers. |
| **MUFAP** (Mutual Funds Association of Pakistan) | Industry association of AMCs, **not a regulator** | Publishes fund data, NAVs and industry standards. Complaints about funds go to the AMC, then SECP. See funds/mufap. |
| **FBR** (Federal Board of Revenue) | Federal tax authority | Income tax, withholding taxes, the Active Taxpayers List. See basics/tax. |
| **CDNS** (Central Directorate of National Savings) | Government savings body under the Ministry of Finance | Sells National Savings certificates and accounts. See savings/national-savings. |
| **DPC** (Deposit Protection Corporation) | Subsidiary of SBP | Protects small bank depositors up to a set amount if a member bank fails. Check its current limit. |
| **PACRA and VIS** | The credit rating companies licensed by SECP | Rate banks, companies, bonds, sukuk and funds. A rating is an opinion, not a guarantee. |
| **Banking Mohtasib Pakistan** | Banking ombudsman | Free dispute route for individual complaints against banks. |
| **Federal Tax Ombudsman** | Tax ombudsman | Complaints about maladministration by FBR officials. |
| **Federal Insurance Ombudsman** | Insurance ombudsman | Complaints against insurance companies. |
| **Wafaqi Mohtasib** (Federal Ombudsman) | General federal ombudsman | Complaints about federal government agencies, such as CDNS. |

There is no separate "securities ombudsman". Complaints about brokers, AMCs and listed companies go to the firm first, then PSX or SECP.

## Main laws and rules
| Law or rule | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan Act, 1997 | Creates SECP and sets its powers. |
| Securities Act, 2015 | Securities markets, brokers, exchanges, public offers, insider trading and market manipulation. |
| Companies Act, 2017 | Companies, shareholders, directors, disclosure. It also bars unauthorised deposit-taking and pyramid schemes. |
| Central Depositories Act, 1997 | The legal base for CDC and electronic holding of securities. |
| Futures Market Act, 2016 | Futures exchanges (PMEX) and futures brokers. |
| Stock Exchanges (Corporatization, Demutualization and Integration) Act, 2012 | The legal base for turning the exchanges into companies. This led to the merger into PSX. |
| Non-Banking Finance Companies (Establishment and Regulation) Rules, 2003 | Licensing of NBFCs, including AMCs. |
| Non-Banking Finance Companies and Notified Entities Regulations, 2008 | Detailed rules for mutual funds, AMCs and other NBFCs. |
| Voluntary Pension System Rules, 2005 | Private pension funds (VPS). |
| Real Estate Investment Trust Regulations, 2022 | REITs and REIT management companies. |
| Securities Brokers (Licensing and Operations) Regulations, 2016 | Licensing and conduct of stock brokers. |
| Securities and Futures Advisers (Licensing and Operations) Regulations, 2017 | Licensing of people who give paid investment advice. |
| Credit Rating Companies Regulations, 2016 | Licensing and conduct of rating companies. |
| Modaraba Companies and Modaraba (Floatation and Control) Ordinance, 1980 | Modarabas and modaraba management companies. |
| Insurance Ordinance, 2000 | Insurance companies. It is also the base for the Insurance Ombudsman. |
| State Bank of Pakistan Act, 1956 | Creates SBP and sets its powers. |
| Banking Companies Ordinance, 1962 | Licensing and regulation of banks. The Banking Mohtasib was added to it later. |
| Deposit Protection Corporation Act, 2016 | Creates the DPC. |
| Foreign Exchange Regulation Act, 1947 | Foreign currency dealings and moving money in or out of Pakistan. |
| Income Tax Ordinance, 2001 | Income tax, including tax on dividends, profit and capital gains. |
| Establishment of the Office of Federal Tax Ombudsman Ordinance, 2000 | Creates the Federal Tax Ombudsman. |
| Federal Ombudsmen Institutional Reform Act, 2013 | Common rules that strengthen the federal ombudsmen. |
| Zakat and Ushr Ordinance, 1980 | Compulsory zakat deduction on listed assets. See legal/inheritance-nomination-zakat. |
| Anti-Money Laundering Act, 2010 | Money laundering offences. It is also the base for the Financial Monitoring Unit (FMU). See legal/kyc-aml-and-market-abuse. |
| Benami Transactions (Prohibition) Act, 2017 | Bans holding property in another person's name to hide the real owner. |
| Succession Act, 1925 | Court succession certificates and letters of administration. |
| Letters of Administration and Succession Certificates Act, 2020 | NADRA route for succession certificates in Islamabad Capital Territory. Provinces have passed similar laws. |

## What this means for you
- **Use only licensed entities.** A company being registered with SECP does not mean it may take deposits or manage your money. It needs the right licence for that activity.
- **Check a licence yourself:**
  - Brokers: the PSX broker list and the SECP licensing pages.
  - AMCs, pension fund managers, insurers, NBFCs, modarabas, rating companies: SECP licensing pages. MUFAP also lists AMCs.
  - PMEX brokers: the PMEX website.
  - Banks, microfinance banks, wallets and exchange companies: SBP's list of regulated institutions.
  - National Savings: only CDNS centres, savings.gov.pk and authorised channels.
- **Paid investment advice needs a licence.** Ask for the adviser's licence and check it on secp.gov.pk.
- **Rulebooks exist and are public.** PSX's rulebook, the PSX Regulations, sets the duties of brokers to customers. Examples: contract notes, account statements, complaint handling and default procedures. Find it under "Legal Framework" on psx.com.pk. SECP publishes all its acts, rules and regulations under "Laws" on secp.gov.pk. Federal laws are on pakistancode.gov.pk.
- **Read the official text, not a summary,** when money or a dispute is involved. Rules are amended often.
