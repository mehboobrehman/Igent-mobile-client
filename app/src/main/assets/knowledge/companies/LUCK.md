**Symbol:** LUCK | **Sector:** Cement / conglomerate

Lucky Cement is Pakistan's largest cement maker, with plants in the North and South and abroad. Through subsidiaries it also owns Lucky Core Industries (LCI), Lucky Motor (Kia assembly), Lucky Electric Power (coal) and other businesses.

**Earnings drivers**
- Domestic cement demand and exports.
- Coal prices and PKR/USD.
- Profits from its subsidiaries: autos, power and chemicals.

It is commonly valued with a sum-of-the-parts approach.
