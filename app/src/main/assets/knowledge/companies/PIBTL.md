**Symbol:** PIBTL | **Sector:** Transport (port terminal)

PIBTL runs a coal, clinker and cement bulk-handling terminal at Port Qasim, Karachi. It is part of the Marine Group.

**Earnings drivers**
- Coal import volumes for power and cement plants: a shift to local Thar or Afghan coal reduces volumes.
- Clinker and cement exports.
- Tariffs, which are dollar-linked.
- Debt repayment.

It is a low-priced share traded heavily by retail investors.
