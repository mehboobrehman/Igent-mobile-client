**Symbol:** SAZEW | **Sector:** Automobile assemblers

Sazgar Engineering assembles Haval SUVs (including hybrids) and BAIC vehicles. It also makes three-wheelers (rickshaws) and wheel rims.

**Earnings drivers**
- SUV demand and new launches, such as hybrid and new-energy vehicles.
- Interest rates and auto financing.
- PKR/USD, because kits are imported.
- Tax incentives for hybrid and electric vehicles in the budget.

It grew very fast in 2024–25, so watch for normalisation.
