**Symbol:** AICL | **Sector:** Insurance

Adamjee Insurance is a large general (non-life) insurer and part of the Nishat Group. It also owns a life insurance subsidiary, Adamjee Life.

**Earnings drivers**
- Growth in gross premiums.
- Claims ratios.
- Investment income on its equity and fixed-income portfolios, so it is sensitive to the PSX and to interest rates.
- Motor and health insurance demand.

**Shariah:** Conventional insurance is generally not Shariah-compliant. Igent reads current KMI index status live from PSX.
