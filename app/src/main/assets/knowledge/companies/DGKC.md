**Symbol:** DGKC | **Sector:** Cement

D.G. Khan Cement is a large cement maker in the Nishat Group. It has plants in the North and South, and owns stakes in other Nishat Group companies.

**Earnings drivers**
- Domestic cement demand and the government development budget (PSDP).
- Exports, from its southern plant.
- Coal prices and PKR/USD.
- Finance costs: the company is relatively leveraged, so it benefits when interest rates fall.
- Dividends from its investments.
