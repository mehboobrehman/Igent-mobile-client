**Symbol:** LCI | **Sector:** Chemicals

Lucky Core Industries, formerly ICI Pakistan, is part of the Lucky/Yunus Brothers group. Its businesses are soda ash, polyester fibre, pharmaceuticals and animal health, and agri and chemicals.

**Earnings drivers**
- Soda ash demand from glass and detergent makers.
- Coal and gas costs.
- Pharma pricing.
- PKR/USD.
- Interest rates.
