**Symbol:** ABOT | **Sector:** Pharmaceuticals

Abbott Pakistan is the local subsidiary of US-based Abbott. It sells branded generic medicines, nutrition products and consumer health products.

**Earnings drivers**
- Drug pricing policy: non-essential medicines were deregulated in 2024, while essential medicines are still price-controlled by DRAP.
- PKR/USD, because most active ingredients are imported.
- Sales volume growth.
- Gross margins.
- Royalties and other payments to the parent.
