**Symbol:** CHCC | **Sector:** Cement

Cherat Cement is a cement maker in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and part of the Ghulam Faruque Group. It serves the northern market and exports to Afghanistan.

**Earnings drivers**
- Cement demand in the North.
- Exports to Afghanistan, which suffer when the border is closed.
- Coal prices and PKR/USD.
- Debt and interest costs.
