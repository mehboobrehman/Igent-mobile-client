**Symbol:** BOP | **Sector:** Commercial banks

The Bank of Punjab is majority-owned by the Government of Punjab. It is a fast-growing conventional bank and handles a lot of provincial government business.

**Earnings drivers**
- Net interest margin.
- Deposits from the provincial government.
- Lending under government schemes.
- Asset quality.
- Capital adequacy.

It is popular with retail traders and has a low share price.
