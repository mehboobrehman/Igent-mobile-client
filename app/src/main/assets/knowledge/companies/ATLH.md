**Symbol:** ATLH | **Sector:** Automobiles (motorcycles)

Atlas Honda assembles Honda motorcycles in Pakistan and is the market leader in that segment.

**Earnings drivers**
- Rural incomes and farm output.
- Consumer financing rates.
- PKR/USD, because parts are imported (although local content is high).
- Prices of steel and other inputs.
- Its pricing power.
- Other income from cash balances.
