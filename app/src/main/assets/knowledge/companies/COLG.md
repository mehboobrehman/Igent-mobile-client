**Symbol:** COLG | **Sector:** Personal care / FMCG

Colgate-Palmolive Pakistan is controlled by the Lakson Group under licence from Colgate. It sells toothpaste, detergents (Bonus, Brite) and personal care products.

**Earnings drivers**
- Consumer volumes.
- Palm oil and other commodity input costs.
- PKR/USD.
- Pricing power.
- Sales tax on consumer goods.

The share price is high and free float is limited.
