**Symbol:** POL | **Sector:** Oil & gas exploration and production

Pakistan Oilfields is an oil-weighted exploration and production company in the Attock Group, with operations in northern Pakistan. It also has LPG sales and stakes in Attock Refinery (ATRL) and others.

**Earnings drivers**
- Oil prices and PKR/USD.
- Production from its main fields.
- Low circular-debt exposure compared with its state-owned peers.

Investors hold it mainly for its high dividend payout.
