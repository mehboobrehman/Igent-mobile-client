**Symbol:** ILP | **Sector:** Textiles (hosiery / apparel exporter)

Interloop is one of the world's largest hosiery makers. It also exports denim and activewear to global brands.

**Earnings drivers**
- Export orders from US and EU brands.
- PKR/USD: a weaker PKR helps, because revenue is in dollars.
- Energy costs.
- The export tax regime in the budget.
- US tariff policy.
- Capacity expansion.
