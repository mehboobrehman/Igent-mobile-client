**Symbol:** NCPL | **Sector:** Power

Nishat Chunian Power operates a roughly 200 MW furnace-oil power plant (check its filings for the exact capacity).

**Earnings drivers**
- Its PPA terms, after the 2024–25 IPP renegotiations moved many 2002-policy plants to "hybrid take-and-pay" or other revised terms.
- Collection of circular-debt receivables.
- Dispatch levels.

Dividends can be large when receivables are collected.
