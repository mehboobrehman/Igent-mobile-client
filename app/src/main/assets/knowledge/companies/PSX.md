**Symbol:** PSX | **Sector:** Financial services (stock exchange)

Pakistan Stock Exchange Ltd is the country's only stock exchange, and it is itself listed. It holds stakes in CDC and NCCPL.

**Earnings drivers**
- Market trading volumes and values: earnings are highly cyclical with market activity.
- Listing fees.
- Income from its investments.
- Interest income on its funds.

It is effectively a leveraged bet on PSX trading activity.
