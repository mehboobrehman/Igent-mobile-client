**Symbol:** BWCL | **Sector:** Cement

Bestway Cement is one of Pakistan's largest cement makers by capacity, mostly in the North. It is owned by the UK-based Bestway Group and also holds a stake in United Bank (UBL).

**Earnings drivers**
- Domestic cement demand, which depends on construction and the government development budget (PSDP).
- Coal and energy costs.
- Retention prices.
- Interest rates.
- Dividends from its UBL stake.
