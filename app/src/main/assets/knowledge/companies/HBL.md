**Symbol:** HBL | **Sector:** Commercial banks

HBL is Pakistan's largest private bank by deposits. The Aga Khan Fund for Economic Development (AKFED) is its controlling shareholder. It has international branches and a growing Islamic banking arm and microfinance unit.

**Earnings drivers**
- Net interest margin, which moves with the policy rate.
- Deposit growth.
- Its government securities book.
- Fee income.
- Costs from its international operations.
- Bank taxation in the budget.
