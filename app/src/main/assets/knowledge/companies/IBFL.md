**Symbol:** IBFL | **Sector:** Synthetic fibre / textiles

Ibrahim Fibres makes polyester staple fibre and yarn. It is part of the Ibrahim Group, which also controls Allied Bank, and holds a stake in ABL.

**Earnings drivers**
- Polyester margins over PTA and MEG inputs, which are oil-linked.
- Anti-dumping duties.
- Textile sector demand.
- Energy costs.
- Dividends from its ABL stake.

It has low free float.
