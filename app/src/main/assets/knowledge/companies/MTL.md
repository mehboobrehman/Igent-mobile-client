**Symbol:** MTL | **Sector:** Automobile assemblers (tractors)

Millat Tractors assembles Massey Ferguson tractors and is Pakistan's largest tractor maker.

**Earnings drivers**
- Farm incomes: crop prices, especially wheat support price policy.
- GST on tractors set in the budget.
- Government tractor subsidy schemes.
- Interest rates.
- PKR/USD.

Investors hold it mainly for its high dividend payouts.
