**Symbol:** UPFL | **Sector:** Food

Unilever Pakistan Foods is a listed Unilever subsidiary that sells food brands such as Knorr and Rafhan.

**Earnings drivers**
- Consumer volumes and pricing.
- Input costs, such as maize, wheat, oils and packaging.
- PKR/USD.
- Royalties to the parent.

The share price is very high and free float is small, so liquidity is low.
