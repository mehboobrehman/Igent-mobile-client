**Symbol:** CNERGY | **Sector:** Refinery / oil marketing

Cnergyico, formerly Byco Petroleum, runs a large refinery complex at Hub in Balochistan and a fuel retail network.

**Earnings drivers**
- Refinery utilisation, which has historically been low.
- Refining margins.
- Government refining policy.
- Debt and finance costs.

It is a low-priced share heavily traded by retail investors, so volatility is high.
