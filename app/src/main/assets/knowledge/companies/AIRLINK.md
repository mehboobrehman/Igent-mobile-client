**Symbol:** AIRLINK | **Sector:** Technology & communication

Air Link distributes mobile phones and also assembles them locally, for example Xiaomi handsets through its subsidiary.

**Earnings drivers**
- Smartphone demand.
- PKR/USD, because components are imported.
- Mobile phone taxes and PTA registration fees set in the budget.
- Import policy and letter-of-credit (LC) availability.
- Distribution margins.
- Finance costs, since it uses a lot of working capital.
