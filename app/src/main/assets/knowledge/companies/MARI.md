**Symbol:** MARI | **Sector:** Oil & gas exploration and production

Mari Energies, formerly Mari Petroleum, is a gas-focused exploration and production company. It operates the Mari field, which supplies fertilizer plants. The Fauji Foundation, OGDC and the Government of Pakistan are major shareholders.

**Earnings drivers**
- Gas production volumes.
- Wellhead gas pricing policy.
- New discoveries, such as Ghazij.
- PKR/USD, because prices are dollar-linked.
- Circular debt exposure: lower than its peers.
- Diversification into minerals and IT/data centres.
