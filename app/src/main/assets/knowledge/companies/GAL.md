**Symbol:** GAL | **Sector:** Automobile assemblers

Ghandhara Automobiles, formerly Ghandhara Nissan, assembles vehicles including JAC trucks and Chery-brand SUVs, according to company disclosures.

**Earnings drivers**
- Auto demand, which is highly sensitive to interest rates and auto financing rules.
- PKR/USD, because kits are imported.
- Auto taxes and duties in the budget.
- New model launches.
