**Symbol:** JDWS | **Sector:** Sugar

JDW Sugar Mills is one of Pakistan's largest sugar producers. It also has bagasse-based power plants and corporate farms.

**Earnings drivers**
- Sugarcane support price.
- Sugar prices.
- Government decisions on sugar exports and imports.
- Crushing volumes.
- Power sales tariffs.
- Finance costs, since it uses a lot of working capital.

The sector is heavily politicised.
