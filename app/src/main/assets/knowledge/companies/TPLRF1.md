**Symbol:** TPLRF1 | **Sector:** REIT

TPL REIT Fund I is a Shariah-compliant development REIT managed by TPL REIT Management. It develops real estate projects in Karachi, such as One Hoshang and Mangrove.

**Earnings drivers**
- Revaluation gains on its projects.
- Sales progress on its developments.
- Project timelines.

Unlike a rental REIT, its income is mostly unrealised until projects are sold, so do not treat it as a stable dividend payer.
