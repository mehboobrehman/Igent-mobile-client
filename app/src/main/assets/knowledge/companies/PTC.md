**Symbol:** PTC | **Sector:** Technology & communication (telecom)

PTCL is the fixed-line, broadband and enterprise telecom operator. It owns Ufone, and in 2025 it acquired Telenor Pakistan subject to regulatory approval (check recent announcements for completion). Etisalat (e&) and the Government of Pakistan are its main shareholders.

**Earnings drivers**
- Broadband and enterprise revenue.
- Mobile consolidation synergies.
- PKR/USD, through dollar debt and equipment costs.
- Finance costs.
- Pension liabilities.
