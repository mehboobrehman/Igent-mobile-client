**Symbol:** SRVI | **Sector:** Leather & footwear / tyres

Service Industries makes footwear, for both domestic retail and export. It also makes motorcycle tyres and tubes, and has a subsidiary that makes passenger-car tyres.

**Earnings drivers**
- Footwear exports and domestic retail demand.
- Tyre demand from the motorcycle and auto sectors.
- Rubber prices and PKR/USD.
- Finance costs.
