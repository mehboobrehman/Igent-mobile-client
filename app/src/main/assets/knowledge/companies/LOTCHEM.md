**Symbol:** LOTCHEM | **Sector:** Chemicals

Lotte Chemical Pakistan is Pakistan's only maker of pure terephthalic acid (PTA), the raw material for polyester.

**Ownership:** Lotte Chemical (Korea) sold its majority stake to a local buyer in 2024–25. Check recent announcements for the current owner and any name change.

**Earnings drivers**
- PTA margins over paraxylene, which are oil-linked.
- Polyester demand.
- Import duty protection.
- Gas and energy costs.
