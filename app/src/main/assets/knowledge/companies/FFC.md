**Symbol:** FFC | **Sector:** Fertilizer

Fauji Fertilizer is Pakistan's largest urea producer and part of the Fauji Foundation. It merged with Fauji Fertilizer Bin Qasim (FFBL), which added DAP production. It also owns stakes in Fauji Cement, Askari Bank, power companies, Mari Energies and others.

**Earnings drivers**
- Urea and DAP volumes and prices.
- Gas pricing.
- International DAP and phosphoric acid prices.
- PKR/USD.
- Dividend income from its associates.

Investors hold it mainly for dividends.
