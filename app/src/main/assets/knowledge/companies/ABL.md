**Symbol:** ABL | **Sector:** Commercial banks

Allied Bank is a large conventional commercial bank. It has a big domestic branch network and is controlled by the Ibrahim Group.

**Earnings drivers**
- Net interest margin, which moves with the SBP policy rate and KIBOR.
- Income from its government securities (PIB and T-bill) book.
- Growth in current and savings account deposits.
- Fee income.
- Credit-loss charges.
- The effective tax rate set in the federal budget, including any super tax.
