**Symbol:** TRG | **Sector:** Technology (holding company)

TRG Pakistan is a holding company. Its main asset is an indirect stake in Afiniti, a US AI customer-call-routing company, held through TRG International.

**Earnings drivers**
- Afiniti's valuation and financial health.
- Corporate governance disputes and litigation within the group.

It reports no meaningful operating earnings of its own. It is a speculative, retail-heavy stock.
