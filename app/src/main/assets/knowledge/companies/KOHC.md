**Symbol:** KOHC | **Sector:** Cement

Kohat Cement is a cement maker based in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. It has a relatively low-cost, low-debt balance sheet.

**Earnings drivers**
- Cement demand in the North.
- Coal and energy costs, including its use of captive and renewable power.
- Retention prices.
- Exports to Afghanistan.
