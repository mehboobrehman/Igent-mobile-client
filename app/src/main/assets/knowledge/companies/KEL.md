**Symbol:** KEL | **Sector:** Power (integrated utility)

K-Electric is Karachi's vertically integrated power utility, covering generation, transmission and distribution. Its majority owner is the KES Power consortium, and ownership disputes are ongoing.

**Earnings drivers**
- The NEPRA multi-year tariff determination.
- Line losses and bill recovery.
- Write-offs.
- Circular-debt payables and receivables with government entities.
- Finance costs.

It is a low-priced share with heavy retail trading.
