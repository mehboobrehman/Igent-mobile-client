**Symbol:** ENGROH | **Sector:** Conglomerate / holding

Engro Holdings was formerly Dawood Hercules Corporation. Under a court-sanctioned scheme effective 1 January 2025, Engro Corporation became its wholly owned subsidiary and was delisted. Engro Corp shareholders received about 2.244 ENGROH shares per share.

**Main businesses**
- Fertilizers (EFERT).
- Petrochemicals (Engro Polymer).
- Thar coal and power.
- Telecom towers (Engro Connect / Enfrashare).
- Energy terminals.

**Earnings drivers:** dividends flowing up from its subsidiaries, especially EFERT. It is valued with a sum-of-the-parts approach and usually trades at a holding-company discount.
