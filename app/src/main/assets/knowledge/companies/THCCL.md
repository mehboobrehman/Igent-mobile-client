**Symbol:** THCCL | **Sector:** Cement

Thatta Cement is a small southern cement maker based in Thatta, Sindh.

**Earnings drivers**
- Cement demand and prices in the South.
- Coal costs and PKR/USD.
- Power costs.
- Utilisation of its small capacity.

It has lower liquidity than the large cement makers.
