**Symbol:** FATIMA | **Sector:** Fertilizer

Fatima Fertilizer is a joint venture of the Fatima Group and Arif Habib Group. It makes urea, CAN, NP and NPK fertilizers at Sadiqabad and other plants.

**Earnings drivers**
- Gas price and supply: part of its gas comes from a dedicated field (Mari), and some plants use system gas or RLNG.
- Its product mix: nitrophosphates sell at higher prices than urea.
- Farm demand.
- Government pricing policy.
