**Symbol:** PAKT | **Sector:** Tobacco

Pakistan Tobacco is a subsidiary of British American Tobacco and sells cigarette brands such as Gold Leaf and Capstan.

**Earnings drivers**
- Federal excise duty on cigarettes set in the budget: big hikes push volumes towards illicit and duty-evaded brands.
- Enforcement of track-and-trace.
- Pricing.
- Exports of leaf and cigarettes.

The share price is high and free float is limited.
