**Symbol:** SYS | **Sector:** Technology (IT services)

Systems Limited is Pakistan's largest listed IT services exporter. It provides software development, digital transformation and business process outsourcing (BPO) to clients in the US, Middle East and Europe.

**Earnings drivers**
- Dollar revenue growth.
- PKR/USD: a weaker PKR boosts its PKR earnings.
- Wage inflation for IT staff.
- The IT export tax regime set in the budget.
- Acquisitions.
- Global IT spending, especially in the Gulf region (GCC).
