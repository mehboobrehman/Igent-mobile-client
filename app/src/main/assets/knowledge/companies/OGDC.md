**Symbol:** OGDC | **Sector:** Oil & gas exploration and production

OGDC is Pakistan's largest exploration and production company by reserves and output. The government is its majority owner.

**Earnings drivers**
- Oil prices (Arab Light / Brent) and PKR/USD, because its prices are dollar-linked.
- Production volumes and reservoir decline.
- Gas pricing policy.
- Circular debt: large receivables from SNGP and SSGC limit its cash flow and dividends.
- Exploration success.
- Reko Diq mining stake.
