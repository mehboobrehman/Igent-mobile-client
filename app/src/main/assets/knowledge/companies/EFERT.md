**Symbol:** EFERT | **Sector:** Fertilizer

Engro Fertilizers is a major urea producer and DAP marketer, and a subsidiary of Engro Holdings.

**Earnings drivers**
- Urea volumes and prices.
- Gas price and supply: it gets feed gas at concessionary pricing from its own field arrangement, and gas pricing changes are a key risk.
- Government urea import and pricing policy.
- GIDC history.
- Farm economics.

Investors hold it mainly for high dividends.
