**Symbol:** NML | **Sector:** Textiles (composite)

Nishat Mills is Pakistan's largest listed composite textile company. It also holds stakes in Nishat Group companies, including MCB, DGKC, Nishat Power and Nishat Hotels.

**Earnings drivers**
- Textile exports.
- Cotton and energy costs.
- PKR/USD.
- The export tax regime in the budget.
- Dividend income from its investments.

It trades below the value of its investment portfolio.
