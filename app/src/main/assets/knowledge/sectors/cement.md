# Cement

Cement is a cyclical sector. It moves with domestic construction, government development spending (PSDP), interest rates and energy costs.

## Example listed companies (tickers)
- **LUCK** (Lucky Cement): historically the largest. Diversified through subsidiaries in chemicals, autos (Kia), power and overseas cement.
- Others include e.g. **BWCL** (Bestway), **DGKC** (D.G. Khan), **MLCF** (Maple Leaf), **FCCL** (Fauji Cement), **CHCC** (Cherat), **PIOC** (Pioneer), **KOHC** (Kohat), **POWER** (Power Cement), **ACPL** (Attock Cement).
- North-based plants supply most domestic demand. South-based plants are closer to Karachi ports and do more exporting.

## Demand data
- **APCMA** (All Pakistan Cement Manufacturers Association) publishes monthly despatches in the first days of each month. It splits them into domestic and export, and north and south.
- This is the main high-frequency indicator for the sector. Compare year-on-year and fiscal-year-to-date, since months are seasonal (monsoon, Ramadan, Eid).
- Igent finds the latest figures with search_news / read_news.

## Earnings drivers
- **Retention price:** the net price per bag or ton after taxes and dealer margins. Northern producers often move prices together, so margins can expand even when volumes are weak.
- **Fuel and power:** coal is the main cost. Sources are imported (e.g. South African), Afghan and local coal. Many plants have added waste-heat recovery, solar and wind to cut grid power costs.
- **Interest rates:** several mid-caps carry heavy expansion debt, so rate hikes hurt them directly. Lower rates also help housing and construction demand. Live rates: query_rates.
- **PKR:** a weaker rupee raises imported coal costs but makes exports more competitive.
- **Taxes:** federal excise duty per ton and sales tax are large parts of the price. Watch every budget for changes.

## What to watch
- Monthly APCMA despatches, by region and domestic/export.
- International coal prices and the PKR.
- PSDP release pace, the policy rate and housing finance measures.
- Announced price increases or cuts per bag.

## Pitfalls
- Volume growth without price discipline does not equal profit growth. Check gross margins.
- Debt-heavy mid-caps are highly geared to rates. Small changes in price or coal cost swing their EPS.
- Consolidated results of companies like LUCK include large non-cement subsidiaries.
