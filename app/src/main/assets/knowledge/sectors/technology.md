# Technology & Communication

PSX's technology sector mixes **IT services exporters**, **telecom operators** and **local hardware/consumer-tech assemblers**. Each group has different drivers, so analyse them separately.

## Example listed companies (tickers)
- **IT exporters:** e.g. **SYS** (Systems Limited, historically the largest; software, digital transformation and BPO), **AVN** (Avanceon, automation), **NETSOL** (leasing/finance software), **TRG** (TRG Pakistan, a holding company whose value rests on unlisted foreign holdings).
- **Telecom:** e.g. **PTC** (PTCL, fixed-line/broadband; parent of Ufone). Check the status of any pending mergers or acquisitions before analysing.
- **Local tech:** e.g. **AIRLINK** (phone distribution and assembly), **OCTOPUS** (Octopus Digital).
- Shariah status varies by company and changes. Check it live.

## Drivers for IT exporters
- **USD revenue:** most revenue is earned in USD from the US, Europe and the Middle East, so a weaker PKR boosts rupee earnings and margins.
- **Client IT budgets:** global tech spending cycles. Slowdowns delay projects.
- **Wage inflation:** salaries are the main cost. Emigration of skilled staff pushes wages up.
- **Tax regime:** IT exports have usually had a concessional final-tax regime on export proceeds. Budgets can change it. Check the latest Finance Act.
- **Internet and policy:** internet disruptions and rules on foreign-currency retention affect delivery and cash.

## Sector data
- SBP publishes monthly IT and ICT services export data in its trade-in-services statistics. PBS and industry bodies use different definitions, so totals differ by source. Compare like with like.
- Freelancer remittances are a separate but related series.
- Igent finds the latest figures with search_news / read_news.

## Drivers for telecom and local tech
- Telecom: tariffs, spectrum auctions, PKR (equipment and licence costs in USD), energy costs and heavy capex.
- Local assemblers (e.g. AIRLINK): import policy, PTA phone registration taxes, consumer demand and the PKR. A weaker rupee hurts them, the opposite of IT exporters.

## What to watch
- Monthly SBP IT export data.
- PKR/USD trend (query_rates).
- Quarterly results of SYS (query_announcements): revenue growth in USD terms, margins and headcount.
- Budget treatment of IT export income and any internet-policy shocks.

## Pitfalls
- In past cycles PSX tech stocks traded at high multiples and were volatile. Do not extrapolate peak growth.
- Adjust for bonus issues and splits before comparing historical share prices.
- TRG's value depends on foreign unlisted assets and has had governance disputes. Treat it as speculative.
- Do not mix IT-exporter logic (helped by a weak PKR) with importers like AIRLINK (hurt by a weak PKR).
