# Event playbooks

How major events usually move Pakistani markets. These are tendencies, not rules. Markets react to the **surprise versus expectations**, not the event itself. General education only, not personal investment advice. Check current data with query_rates, query_index and search_news.

Shorthand: up = tends to rise, down = tends to fall, mixed = depends.

## SBP monetary policy decision
- **What/when:** the MPC sets the policy rate on a calendar SBP publishes in advance (macro/policy-rate).
- **Watch:** brokerage polls, T-bill auction yields before the meeting, CPI, real rate, statement tone.
- **Cut bigger than expected:** KSE-100 up, led by leveraged sectors (cement, autos, steel, textiles). Banks mixed to down on margins. T-bill and bond yields down. Income and bond funds get NAV gains, then lower yields. Money market yields drift down. Bank deposit and NSS rates fall with a lag. PKR may weaken slightly.
- **Hike or hawkish surprise:** the reverse.
- **Caveat:** a well-flagged move is often priced in. The guidance in the statement can matter more than the move.

## Federal budget
- **What/when:** presented each year, usually in June, for a tax year starting 1 July (basics/tax).
- **Watch:** capital gains and dividend tax changes, super tax, bank-specific taxes, sector duties and tariffs, petroleum levy, NSS and bank profit tax, the fiscal deficit target and IMF alignment.
- **Typical impact:** higher CGT or dividend tax hits the whole market. New bank taxes hit banks. Duty cuts help the sector concerned (e.g. autos or steel inputs). Bigger tax on bank profit or NSS lowers the after-tax safe rate, which can push money to stocks.
- **Caveat:** proposals change before the Finance Act passes. Read the final law.

## IMF programmes and reviews
- **What/when:** loan programmes with periodic reviews, staff-level agreements and Board approvals.
- **Watch:** staff-level agreement news, prior actions (tariffs, taxes, exchange rate), Board dates.
- **Successful review:** KSE-100 up, PKR firmer, yields and default risk down, rating upgrades more likely.
- **Delay or breakdown:** sharp sell-off, PKR pressure, yields up, gold in PKR up.
- **Caveat:** IMF conditions (energy tariffs, new taxes) can hurt particular sectors even when the deal helps the market.

## Elections and political crises
- **What/when:** general elections roughly every five years; protests, sit-ins and court cases at any time.
- **Typical impact:** uncertainty before; relief rally if a clear, stable result emerges. Prolonged unrest: KSE-100 down, PKR weaker, gold up, investors move to money market funds.
- **Caveat:** the market often looks through short protests. Policy continuity and IMF compliance matter more than who wins.

## Oil price jumps
- **Watch:** Brent, Middle East news, petrol price revisions.
- **Typical impact:** Pakistan imports oil, so the import bill and inflation rise. PKR under pressure. E&P stocks up (sectors/oil-gas-exploration). Refiners and OMCs may book inventory gains (sectors/oil-marketing). Most other sectors and KSE-100 overall down. Rate-cut hopes fade, yields up.
- **Oil price fall:** usually the reverse.

## Sharp PKR devaluation or moves
- See macro/dollar.
- **Weaker PKR:** exporters up (IT, textiles; sectors/technology, sectors/textiles), E&P up (dollar-linked revenue). Importers and firms with dollar debt down (autos, some pharma, OMCs). Inflation risk up, so rate-cut hopes fall. Gold in PKR up (macro/gold).
- **Caveat:** a controlled, IMF-backed adjustment can calm markets. A disorderly fall usually brings rate hikes.

## Inflation prints
- **When:** PBS releases CPI monthly, usually early in the month, and SPI weekly (macro/inflation).
- **Lower than expected:** rate-cut hopes rise, stocks and bonds up, yields down.
- **Higher than expected:** the reverse.
- **Caveat:** base effects distort y/y. Core inflation matters to SBP.

## Credit rating actions (Moody's, S&P, Fitch)
- **Upgrade or positive outlook:** foreign interest, Eurobond yields down, KSE-100 mildly up, PKR firmer.
- **Downgrade:** the reverse; banks are exposed because they hold large amounts of government debt.
- **Caveat:** ratings often follow events (IMF deals, reserves) the market already knows.

## Global risk-off
- **Examples:** global recession fear, war, big moves in US rates.
- **Typical impact:** foreign selling hits large caps, oil may fall (helpful) or spike (harmful), gold up in USD and PKR.

## Circular debt and energy tariff decisions
- **What:** NEPRA tariff determinations, monthly fuel charge adjustments, gas price notifications, circular debt settlement plans.
- **Typical impact:** settlement plans and payments help E&Ps, power IPPs, gas utilities and OMCs (sectors/power), and can unlock dividends. Tariff hikes raise CPI short-term and hurt energy-heavy industries (textiles, cement, fertilizer).
- **Caveat:** many plans are announced and partly delivered. Wait for cash.

## Floods and natural disasters
- **Typical impact:** crop losses (cotton, wheat), food inflation, higher imports, fiscal strain, aid inflows. Fertilizer and textiles hurt near term; cement may later benefit from rebuilding. PKR and rate-cut hopes come under pressure.

| Political crisis | Down | Up | Mixed | Up | Down |
| Oil spike | Down (E&P up) | Up | Down | Up | Down |

## Playbook rules
1. **Act on surprises, not scheduled news.** If everyone expected it, it is likely priced in.
2. **Compare with consensus** (polls, T-bill yields, analyst previews) before judging.
3. **Size small under uncertainty.** Lower confidence on idea cards when the outcome is binary.
4. **Always compare with the safe rate** after tax (savings/safe-rates). In high-rate periods, doing nothing is a real option.
5. **Prefer funds over single stocks when uncertain** (funds/fund-types).
6. **Second-round effects matter.** A PKR fall today can mean hikes later.
7. **Do not chase gaps.** Big opening moves often partly reverse.
8. **Check the sector link** in sectors/* before tagging winners and losers.
