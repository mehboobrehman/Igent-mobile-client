# ETFs on PSX

ETFs are open-end index funds whose units trade on PSX like shares. You buy them through a broker and hold them in CDC. The first PSX ETFs launched in late 2020. PSX now lists several ETFs, and new ones launch or close over time. Check the PSX Data Portal (dps.psx.com.pk) for the current list.

## Types you will find
- **Broad or large-cap equity trackers:** track a narrow basket of the biggest KSE-100 stocks by free float.
- **Shariah equity trackers:** track an Islamic index built from KMI stocks. Example: MZNPETF (Meezan), which tracks the Meezan Pakistan Index.
- **Factor or sector trackers:** for example momentum, consumer or banking indices.
- **Fixed income / treasury ETFs:** hold government securities. Example: HBLTETF (HBL).

These are examples only, not a complete or current list. Each ETF's tracker index is described on the PSX indices page and in the AMC's fund documents. Check whether a gold ETF exists before saying one does or does not.

## How to analyse an ETF
- **Price vs iNAV/NAV:** the market price can trade at a premium or discount to NAV, especially when trading is thin. Check the NAV (query_navs or the AMC site) before placing a limit order.
- **Tracking error:** compare the ETF's return with its index over 1 year. Fees (TER) and cash drag cause gaps.
- **Concentration:** narrow trackers (9–15 stocks) are far more concentrated than KSE-100. A few sectors usually dominate.
- **Costs:** you pay broker commission and fees to trade, plus the fund's TER inside NAV. There is no front load.
- **Tax:** CGT and distributions are taxed under the rules for listed securities or fund units. Rates change each budget; see basics/tax and confirm with NCCPL or the AMC.

## Liquidity caveats
- Many PSX ETFs trade very low daily volumes, and spreads can be wide. Some ETFs have kept larger lot sizes even after PSX moved to one-share lots in 2024. Check the lot size before ordering.
- Authorised participants and market makers exist, but quotes are not always live. Always use **limit orders**.
- For larger sums, an AMC index fund or equity fund may be easier to enter and exit at NAV.

## When Igent might suggest an ETF
- Low-cost, rules-based equity exposure for a long horizon.
- Shariah investors wanting a passive option: a Shariah-index ETF.
- Not for cash parking. Use money market funds instead.
