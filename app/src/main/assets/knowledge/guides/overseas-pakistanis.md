# Investing in Pakistan from abroad

Overseas Pakistanis can invest in Pakistan without visiting. The main route is the **Roshan Digital Account (RDA)**, an SBP initiative launched in September 2020 with Pakistani banks. Background: basics/accounts.

Rules, eligible products and limits change. Always confirm on sbp.org.pk and with the bank before acting.

## Who can open an RDA (general)
- Non-resident Pakistanis holding a CNIC or NICOP.
- Persons of Pakistani origin holding a Pakistan Origin Card (POC), under the bank's rules.
- Some banks allow joint accounts with another eligible person.
- Each participating bank sets its own exact eligibility and documents. Check SBP's list of participating banks on sbp.org.pk.

## Step 1: Prepare documents
- Valid CNIC, NICOP or POC.
- Passport, and proof of residence abroad (for example visa, residence permit or utility bill), as the bank asks.
- Proof of income or source of funds abroad.
- Tax residency details for the country where you live (banks collect this for international reporting).
- Mobile number and email you control.

## Step 2: Choose a participating bank
Compare (no bank is recommended here):
- Ease of the online application and app quality.
- Which investment routes are linked (brokers, AMCs, certificates, property).
- Fees and currency conversion spreads.
- Customer support hours in your time zone.

## Step 3: Open the RDA online
1. Use the bank's official website or app. Reach it through the SBP list of participating banks, not through ads.
2. Fill the digital form and upload documents.
3. Complete identity verification (video call, biometric or NADRA verification, as the bank uses).
4. Choose account types: a foreign currency account and/or a PKR account.
5. Receive your account details and IBAN.

## Step 4: Fund the account
- Transfer from your own bank account abroad through normal banking channels.
- Use your own name on both sides. Keep transfer records.
- Never send money through hawala/hundi or through a person's account for "investment". It breaks the audit trail you need for repatriation and tax.

## Step 5: Choose investments
From an RDA you can generally access:

| Option | How it works (general) | Learn more |
|---|---|---|
| Naya Pakistan Certificates (NPCs) | Government of Pakistan certificates in foreign currencies and PKR, in conventional and Islamic versions. Rates and tenors set by the government. | Ask the bank; check sbp.org.pk for current terms |
| PSX shares | The bank links your RDA to a broker and a CDC account so you can trade listed shares | guides/stocks-step-by-step |
| Mutual funds | Invest with AMCs through the RDA link | guides/mutual-funds-step-by-step |
| Property | Buy property in Pakistan with RDA funds, and some banks offer home and car finance schemes for RDA holders | Ask the bank for current options |
| Bank deposits | Term deposits in PKR or foreign currency at the RDA bank | guides/bank-deposits-step-by-step |

Steps for PSX or funds:
1. In the bank app, choose the equity or mutual fund option.
2. Select a broker or AMC from those linked to the bank.
3. Complete the broker or AMC's KYC and risk profile.
4. Turn on CDC SMS/e-alerts and CDC online access (guides/stocks-step-by-step).
5. Fund the broker or AMC only through the RDA.

Consider currency risk: if the rupee weakens, PKR investments lose value in your home currency. Compare PKR returns after expected rupee depreciation (see macro/dollar). Foreign currency NPCs avoid PKR risk but usually pay less.

Example (made-up numbers): a PKR investment earns 12% in a year, but the rupee falls 8% against your currency. Your return in your home currency is about 1.12 / 1.08 - 1 = 3.7%.

## Step 6: Repatriation (sending money back)
- A key feature of the RDA is that funds and investment proceeds are repatriable abroad under SBP rules, without needing a separate approval.
- Keep all records: inward transfers, contract notes, fund statements, certificate confirmations.
- Proceeds from PSX or funds return to your RDA first. Then you instruct the bank to transfer abroad.
- Check with the bank for current rules, timelines and charges.

## Step 7: Tax status
- Tax on profit, dividends and capital gains is usually withheld at source in Pakistan, like for residents (see basics/tax).
- Whether you must file a Pakistani return depends on your residency status (based on days spent in Pakistan in the tax year) and your Pakistani income. Rules for RDA holders and non-residents have changed over time.
- Some withholding rates depend on whether you are on the Active Taxpayers List. Check how the current law treats non-resident and RDA holders on fbr.gov.pk or with a tax adviser.
- You may also owe tax in your country of residence. Tax treaties may give credit for Pakistani tax. Ask a tax adviser in that country.
- See guides/become-a-filer if you need to file in Pakistan.

## Step 8: Keep your records and CNIC/NICOP valid
- Renew your NICOP/CNIC before it expires. Expired IDs can freeze accounts, block dividends and delay transactions.
- Update your address, phone and email with the bank, broker, AMC and CDC.
- Name nominees where offered. Tell family where records are.

## Not using an RDA?
- Non-residents can also use regular non-resident accounts and some direct products. Rules differ by product. Ask the bank or AMC.
- Family in Pakistan Investing on your behalf should use accounts in **their own name with their own documented funds**, or yours. Avoid informal arrangements.

## Red flags
- "Agents" promising to invest your remittances for fixed high profit.
- Property offers in unapproved housing schemes. Check approvals with the relevant development authority.
- Apps or websites claiming to be RDA banks but not on SBP's list.
- Requests to send funds to personal accounts.
See guides/complaints-and-safety.
