# Safety Net (My Money > Safety Net)

An optional page where you tell Igent about money that is not your investable cash. Igent uses it to judge how much risk is right for you. It stays on this PC.

## Why it exists
- **Cash on Hand** (Holdings tab) is the money you could invest now. Igent does not know what else you have.
- Someone with no savings should not take the same risks as someone with a full safety cushion. The Safety Net lets the agent and Chat tell the difference.

## The fields (all optional, rough numbers are fine)
- **Money in the Bank to Keep Safe**: savings you will not invest. Separate from Cash on Hand.
- **Monthly Expenses**: rent, food, bills, school, instalments.
- **Average Monthly Income, After Tax**: salary, or the average for a business. Used to work out your monthly surplus.
- **Debt You Owe** and **Debt Cost per Year (%)**: loans or credit card. A high rate means paying the debt off can beat investing.
- **Gold You Own (Tola)** and **Silver You Own (Tola)**: jewellery or bars. Gold you recorded on the Holdings tab is already counted there; do not add it twice.
- **Property, Rental or Land**: one rough rupee number. Leave out the home you live in.
- **Your Age** and **Retire at Age**: the years left to compound. Igent counts years to retirement (shown in **Your Safety**).
- Press **Save**. The card shows "saved <date>". Update it when things change.

## What you see
- **Your Safety**: months of expenses covered = bank safety money divided by monthly expenses. **Low** is under 3 months, **OK** is 3 to 6, **Strong** is 6 or more. Without monthly expenses it asks you to add them.
- Under it, once income and expenses are both entered: **Monthly surplus** (income minus expenses) and **Savings rate** (surplus as a percent of income). A negative surplus is shown in red with a note to fix spending before investing.
- **Where My Wealth Sits**: cash to invest, holdings, bank, gold owned and property, each with a rupee amount and a share. **Worth after debt** is the total minus your debt. Owned gold uses Igent's 24K gold price. Silver is not valued because Igent has no silver price.

## How Igent uses it
- **Daily agent and Chat** receive a short summary. It includes the monthly surplus and savings rate, so the agent can suggest monthly amounts that fit; the income number itself is not written into it. When safety is **Low** or unknown, safe options (T-bills, money market funds, National Savings) come first. With a high-rate debt, paying it off is mentioned. When gold or property is already a big share, more of it is not suggested. The agent never asks you for more and never gives advice on property or vehicles.
- **Age** is a guide only. With 20 or more years to retirement, growth assets can have a larger share. With under 10, safe and steady options lean first. A goal that is due soon still needs safe money, whatever your age. The agent and Chat are told your age, retirement age and years left.
- **Retirement goal**: when age, retirement age and monthly expenses are saved, **+ Retirement Goal** appears on the Goals tab. It fills the cost as 25 times a year of expenses (a common rule of thumb, a pot you can draw about 4% from each year), in today's rupees, and the date from your retirement age. Change both as you like.
- **Where Should My Next Rupees Go?** (Tools) starts "Money Can Stay Invested" at **5+ years** when more than 5 years remain to retirement. Pick less for a nearer goal. It sets its emergency fund question from your Safety Net: 3 months or more means "I already have an emergency fund". Less fills in your monthly expenses. You can change both.
- **Zakat tab**: the bank, gold and silver figures fill the boxes you leave empty. Type your own number to override. (Zakat's debts box is for debts due now, so it is not filled.)
- Nothing is entered until you save. If you never fill it in, nothing changes and the agent is told it is not entered.

## Notes
- It is not a full asset list. A car, furniture and the home you live in are not investments, so they are left out.
- Income is not sent as a number, but the AI is given your expenses and your surplus, so it could work your income out. Leave income empty if you do not want that.
- The figures are your own and may be old. Igent shows the date saved but does not check the numbers.
- **Reset Igent** (Settings > Backups) deletes it with the other settings.
