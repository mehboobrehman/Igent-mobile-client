# Learn page
A course that takes an absolute beginner to making their own investment decisions, made for Pakistan. Three tabs:
- **Course**: 29 short lessons in 4 levels: **Level 1 · First Steps** (saving vs investing, emergency fund, risk, safe options, mutual funds, gold and dollar, tax and filer status, a first plan), **Level 2 · Getting Into the Market** (shares and PSX, opening an account, orders and costs, indices, dividends/bonus/rights, diversification, Shariah investing, cost averaging), **Level 3 · Analysing Investments** (reading results, valuation, sectors, macro, REITs/ETFs/VPS, news and events, mistakes and scams) and **Level 4 · Making Your Own Decisions** (investment thesis, position sizing, building a portfolio, when to sell, beating the safe rate, decision checklist and journal).
  - The left side shows **Your Progress** (lessons done), the lesson list with ✓ for done and • for opened, and a **Take the level quiz →** link when a level is finished.
  - **Continue** opens the lesson where you stopped. Opening Learn also resumes there by itself.
  - **Start Over** (then **Yes, Start Over**) clears lesson progress. Quiz history and the practice portfolio are kept.
  - Each lesson has the text with a Pakistani example, **Practice** calculators (compound growth, inflation, emergency fund, after-tax return vs the safe rate, goal planner, trading costs, dividend yield, asset allocation, rupee cost averaging, quick valuation, position size), **Check Yourself** questions (pick answers, then **Check Answers**; **Try Again** resets them), **Read More** links to knowledge-pack notes, **← Back**, **Ask Igent About This** (opens a chat that explains the lesson again) and **Mark Done & Next →**.
  - Calculator numbers are examples, not current rates; take current figures from the Dashboard or ask Igent.
- **Practice**: paper trading. You start with Rs 1,000,000 of pretend money. Search **Shares** (symbol or company) or **Mutual Funds** (fund, AMC or category), choose **Buy** or **Sell**, enter shares or units (or an amount in rupees when buying a fund), add a one-line reason, then click **Buy (Practice)** / **Sell (Practice)**.
  - Trades fill at Igent's last saved PSX closing price or fund NAV (end of day, not live). Share trades include an example cost of 0.2% each way; funds have none.
  - Cards show **Total Value**, **Practice Cash**, **Invested**, **Your Gain** and **KSE-100 Since Your First Trade** (to compare yourself with the market). **Practice Holdings** lists each holding with average cost, latest price, value, gain and a **Sell** shortcut. **Trade History** lists trades with your reasons. **Reset Practice** (then **Yes, Reset**) starts again with Rs 1,000,000.
  - A share or fund with no saved price yet cannot be traded. Practice never touches your real cash or holdings on My Money.
- **Quiz**: choose a **Level** (one of the four, or **Mixed (Whole Course)**) and the number of **Questions** (5, 10, 15 or 20), then **Start Quiz**. The AI writes new questions from that level's lessons each time: ideas, small sums and real-life decisions. Without an AI model, Igent uses the questions from the lessons.
  - Answer one question at a time: **Check** shows right or wrong with the reason; **Skip** moves on; **Quit Quiz** stops. At the end you see your score, a review of every question, and **Review the lesson →** links for the ones you missed. **Past Quizzes** lists earlier scores.
  - **Review My Mistakes** (in the Level list, as a card on the Quiz tab, and a button after a quiz with wrong answers): up to half the questions are ones you missed or skipped, asked again with the options reshuffled; the rest are new questions on those lessons and on lessons where Check Yourself was not full marks. A question leaves the list once you answer it right.
