# My Money, price alerts, Tools and the Weekly Summary

## My Money
Three tabs: **Holdings**, **Goals** and **Zakat**. Everything stays on this PC.

### Holdings
- **Tiles**: Cash on Hand, Holdings Value (cost and gain %), Total.
- **Update Cash on Hand**: type an amount, then **Save**. A short history is listed below.
- **Record a Buy**: Type (**Stock**, **Fund**, **Gold**, **Savings**), name, quantity, price paid per unit, **Costs (Rs)** (broker commission and charges, optional), **Bought On**, then **Add**.
  - A second buy of the same stock, fund or gold joins the same holding at the **average cost** (total paid including costs ÷ units).
  - Each savings buy (dsc, bsc, ric, sa, sisa, sita) stays separate, because its profit counts from its own date. Quantity is 1 and the price is the amount invested.
- **Holdings** table: Holding, Qty, Avg Cost, Cost, Now (latest price and date), Value, Gain, and buttons:
  - **Sell** (**Encash** for savings): quantity (part or all), sale price per unit (savings: amount received), costs, **Tax Taken** (capital gains tax already deducted, for example by NCCPL for shares or the fund company for funds), date, then **Save**. It shows the gain on the sale before you save.
  - **Dividend** (stocks and funds): amount before tax, tax withheld, date. Both are on the dividend statement.
  - **Remove**: deletes the holding and all its history. Use it only for a wrong entry; for a real sale use Sell.
- **Gains This Tax Year** (Pakistan's tax year runs 1 July to 30 June): Realised Gain (sales minus the average cost and costs), Dividends (before tax), Tax Taken at Source, and **Capital Gains Tax Still Due (Estimate)**. Type your own capital gains tax rate in the small box and click **Save**. The rate changes with the budget and filer status, so check it on the FBR website or ask Chat. It is an estimate, not tax advice.
- **History**: every buy, sell, encashment and dividend, newest first, with costs, tax and the gain on each sale. **✕** deletes one entry; holdings are then worked out again. **Show All** lists everything.
- Holdings added before this version were kept as opening buys.

### Goals
- **+ New Goal**: name (for example Hajj, House, Child's Education), cost in today's rupees, target date, already saved, how much you invest per month, expected return per year and inflation per year. The return and inflation boxes start from the 3-month T-bill and the latest CPI inflation when Igent has them.
- Each goal shows **on track** or **behind**, a progress bar, the cost then (with inflation), what you will have, the monthly amount needed, and how much to add per month. **Edit** and **Delete** change it.
- **+ Retirement Goal** appears when the Safety Net has your age, retirement age and monthly expenses (app/safety-net). It fills in the cost (25 times a year of expenses) and the date. Change both as you like.
- For goals less than about 3 years away, use the safe rate as the return, because shares can be down when the money is needed.

### Zakat
- **From My Money**: cash on hand, gold, shares, mutual funds and savings, at the latest prices.
- **Add What Is Not in My Money**: other cash and bank balances, other gold such as jewellery (tola), silver (tola), money owed to you that will likely be paid, and debts due now. Empty bank, gold and silver boxes use your figures from the Safety Net tab (app/safety-net); type a number to override.
- **Choices**: **Nisab Based On** silver (52.5 tola, most used) or gold (7.5 tola); the **silver price per tola** (Igent has no silver feed, so type today's rate); the gold price (Igent's 24K rate is used when empty); **Shares and Funds** at full market value or a part you choose; **Count savings schemes** (turn off if zakat was already deducted at source).
- **Your Zakat** shows zakatable wealth, the nisab and the zakat due (2.5%), or that the wealth is below the nisab. Your entries are kept on this PC for next year.
- It is a calculator, not a fatwa. Ask a scholar for your own case.

## Price alerts (Watchlist)
- Each watchlist row has **Alert Below** and **Alert Above** price boxes. Type a price and click away to save; empty clears it.
- When a stock's close (or a fund's NAV) reaches the price, Igent shows one toast (even if big-move alerts are off; quiet hours still apply) and the row shows **target hit**. Hover it for the detail. Saving a new price clears the mark, so the alert works again.
- Prices are end-of-day, so alerts come after the PSX close report, not during trading.

## Tools
Sidebar **Tools**. A menu on the left groups 17 tools. Six more are in app/tools-more (Stock Screener, Fair Value Check, Trade Cost and Break-Even, Pay Debt or Invest, Rebalance Helper, Dividend Income). All use Igent's stored data and your My Money entries; they are analysis, not advice.

**Decide**
- **Should I Buy?**: type a PSX symbol, optionally the amount and how long the money can stay invested, then **Check**. Igent runs the 13-step decision checklist from Learn Level 4 with stored results, prices, dividends, news, the safe rate and your money, goals and settings. Verdict **Yes, it passes**, **Not yet: wait** or **No**; hurdle, expected return, a suggested amount (never above your max position), each step ✓ / ✕ / ?, a thesis, what could go wrong. Needs an AI model.
- **Should I Sell?**: pick a share or fund you hold, optionally say why, then **Check**. Igent shows the facts (value, gain, share of your money against your cap, held since, tax estimate), flags (over the cap, a goal coming up, a book closure soon, down more than 20%) and goes through the sell checklist from the lesson "When to sell": thesis broken, better opportunity, overvalued, over the cap, needing the money soon, or only noise. It uses the notes you wrote when buying. Verdict **Hold**, **Trim (sell part)** with a suggested % or **Sell**. Needs an AI model.
- **Compare Two**: choose Shares or Mutual Funds, pick A and B (suggestions appear as you type), optionally what the money is for, then **Compare**. A side-by-side table from stored data (green marks the better number on a line); with an AI model, a verdict on which fits you better and who each suits.
- **Fund Picker**: choose how long the money can stay invested and the ups and downs you accept, Shariah only (starts from your setting) and whether to include pension (VPS) funds, then **Find Funds**. Igent ranks real MUFAP funds of the right types (money market, income, mixed, equity) by return per year over its stored prices, with a penalty for big ups and downs for share funds. Fixed-rate funds are left out. Short history can mislead: check each fund's report and long-term record.

**Plan Ahead**
- **Next Rupees**: enter an amount and how long it can stay invested, and say whether you have an emergency fund (if not, your monthly expenses). Igent fills the emergency fund first, then splits the rest between safe options, funds and shares by time, names real funds and your watchlist shares, and moves the share part to funds if you already hold many shares.
- **SIP Calculator**: type a monthly amount, years, return per year, a **Yearly Rise (%)** (the monthly amount goes up that much each year, like a pay raise) and inflation. Return starts from the safe rate and inflation from the latest CPI. It shows what you pay in, growth, the future value, the value in today's rupees, the gain from the yearly rise and a year-by-year table. No AI. The return is your guess. Fees and tax are not counted. Chat and the agent can run the same sums with the calculate tool (op sip).
- **What If?**: click a preset (market crash, rupee falls, gold jumps, rates up, strong rally) or type moves for KSE-100, the dollar, world gold and interest rates. It shows the rupee change for each holding and the total, and how each was worked out (price history or a sector rule of thumb). A rough guide, not a forecast.

**Review**
- **Portfolio Check-Up**: opens straight away. A health score out of 100, the top 3 fixes, where your money is, shares by sector, and findings (one share over your cap, too much in one sector, idle cash and what it could earn, goals behind, a near goal funded by shares, non-Shariah holdings when Shariah-only is on, too much gold, big losers, missing prices). Rules only, no AI.
- **Journal Review**: choose your real trades (My Money) or practice trades (Learn), then **Review**. Stats (trades, wins and losses, average win and loss, trades with a note) and habits: buying after big rises, selling winners early, holding losers, very busy months, losses bigger than wins, missing notes. With an AI model, a short coach's note. Some checks need more price history.

**Stay Safe**
- **Scam Check**: paste an offer or message, then **Check This Offer** (see below).
- **Tip Checker**: paste a share tip, then **Check This Tip**. Igent finds the PSX symbols and any target price, and checks each share for pump-and-dump signs: small company, low free float, already up a lot, near its 52-week high, a volume spike, losses, very high P/E, outside the KSE-100, a big target. It also checks the wording (hype, insider claims, sure-shot, paid groups, pressure). **Run Should I Buy?** checks the share properly.

### Scam Check details
Paste a WhatsApp message, SMS, advert or offer. Built-in checks always run (guaranteed or very high returns, doubling money, referral income, pressure to hurry, payment to a personal account or mobile wallet, crypto or forex signals, claimed approval, secrecy, OTP or CNIC requests). With an AI model Igent also reads it like an expert. Result: **Likely a scam**, **Suspicious** or **No clear warning signs**, the warning signs with reasons, and checks before paying.

## Weekly Summary
- Every Friday at 18:30 Igent makes a summary of the last 7 days and shows a toast. If Igent was off, it makes it when Igent next starts (Friday evening to Sunday).
- Open it from the toast or **Weekly Summary →** at the top right of the Dashboard. **Make Now** builds one for the last 7 days; the dropdown shows past weeks.
- It shows Igent's short note (with an AI model), your money and the week's movers, the market week (KSE-100, dollar, gold, policy rate, T-bill), new and scored idea cards, alerts, what is coming up next week, and your next lesson with a button to review missed quiz questions.
